About Blake Giunta

Blake Giunta is a Christian speaker and the founder of BeliefMap, a resource that organizes arguments about God’s existence and Jesus’ resurrection. His public work includes debates, podcast discussions, and teaching at churches, university campuses, and apologetics conferences.
