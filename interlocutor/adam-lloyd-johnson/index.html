About Adam Lloyd Johnson

Adam Lloyd Johnson is a Christian author and speaker whose work examines philosophy of religion and the foundations of morality. He earned a doctorate in theological studies at Southeastern Baptist Theological Seminary and wrote Divine Love Theory.
