About Massimo Pigliucci

Massimo Pigliucci is a philosopher and evolutionary biologist whose work examines philosophy of science, pseudoscience, and practical philosophy. His books include How to Be a Stoic and Nonsense on Stilts, and he teaches at the City College of New York.
