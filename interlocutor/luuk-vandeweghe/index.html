About Luuk Vandeweghe

Luuk Vandeweghe is a Christian author and New Testament researcher whose work examines the historical reliability of the Gospels. His books include The Historical Tell and Living Footnotes in the Gospel of Luke, focusing on eyewitness testimony and Luke–Acts.
