About Dennis Nørmark

Dennis Nørmark is an anthropologist, author, speaker, and consultant whose work examines culture, working life, and leadership. His books include Pseudoarbejde and Ufrihedens pris, and he co-wrote Gid min chef var høvding with anthropologist Christian Groes.
