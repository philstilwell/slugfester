About Anthony Kenny

Anthony Kenny’s work spans the history of philosophy, mind, and religion. His books introduce and examine major thinkers, especially Aristotle, Thomas Aquinas, and Wittgenstein, combining historical interpretation with philosophical analysis.

Richard Dawkins, Rowan Williams & Anthony Kenny: Humanity's Ultimate Origins · 2012

Williams presents humanity as the universe becoming linguistically and morally self-aware within a divine relation; Dawkins argues that natural selection explains apparent purpose without design; Kenny presses both accounts from an explicitly agnostic philosophical position.
