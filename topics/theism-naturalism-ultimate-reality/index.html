About this topic

Comparisons of whole worldviews: whether theism, naturalism, or other accounts best explain reality, consciousness, value, and experience. The focus is comparative explanatory scope, simplicity, and evidence, rather than a creator-only argument or one divine attribute.

Debates are grouped by their main question. Scores assess the reasoning presented, not the truth of a position. Read the assessment method.

Jonathan McLatchie vs Tom Jump — Does God Exist? · 2019

McLatchie’s cumulative Bayesian case narrowly prevailed, while Jump’s underdetermination challenges exposed unresolved comparisons behind its numerical confidence.

Christopher Hitchens vs Douglas Wilson — Truth, Goodness, and Beauty · 2008

Wilson argues that theism grounds reason, value, and purposeful beauty; Hitchens answers with human responsibility, secular awe, skeptical inquiry, and stricter tests for miracles.

Douglas Wilson: 75 · Christopher Hitchens: 80. Scores assess the reasoning in this debate.
