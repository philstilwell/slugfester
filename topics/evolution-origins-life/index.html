James Tour vs S. Joshua Swamidass: How Close Is Origin-of-Life Science? · 2020

Tour demands an integrated chemical pathway to first life, while Swamidass defends limited clues, probabilistic uncertainty, empirical testing, and scientific agnosticism.

James Tour: 80 · S. Joshua Swamidass: 78. Scores assess the reasoning in this debate.

Michael Ruse vs Fuz Rana: Evolution or Design at Life's Origin? · 2013

Ruse defended cumulative chemical and evolutionary explanations, while Rana pressed chemical constraints, cellular integration, testability, and positive indicators of intelligent agency.

Michael Ruse: 82 · Fuz Rana: 81. Scores assess the reasoning in this debate.

James Tour vs Lee Cronin: Are We Close to Discovering the Origin of Life? · 2020

Tour pressed unresolved cellular thresholds and evidential overstatement; Cronin defended experimentally testable chemical organization while carefully conceding uncertainty about historical sequence.

James Tour: 82 · Lee Cronin: 80. Scores assess the reasoning in this debate.

Richard Dawkins, Rowan Williams & Anthony Kenny: Humanity's Ultimate Origins · 2012

Williams presents humanity as the universe becoming linguistically and morally self-aware within a divine relation; Dawkins argues that natural selection explains apparent purpose without design; Kenny presses both accounts from an explicitly agnostic philosophical position.

Rowan Williams: 86 · Richard Dawkins and Anthony Kenny: 90. Scores assess the reasoning in this debate.

Alvin Plantinga vs Daniel Dennett: Science, Religion, and Evolution · 2018

Plantinga establishes evolutionary compatibility with divine guidance, while Dennett separates possibility from evidence and offers a narrowly stronger naturalistic account of cognitive reliability.

Alvin Plantinga: 83 · Daniel Dennett: 84. Scores assess the reasoning in this debate.

Dave Farina vs James Tour: Are We Clueless About the Origin of Life? · 2023

Tour pressed unresolved selectivity, replication, information, and integration barriers; Farina answered with experimental component mechanisms while distinguishing incomplete reconstruction from scientific cluelessness.

James Tour: 80 · Dave Farina: 79. Scores assess the reasoning in this debate.

James Tour vs Sy Garte: Evolution, Agency, and Christian Faith · 2025

Tour demands mechanisms for major transitions, while Garte defends a bounded evolutionary framework compatible with Christian unity, agency research, and acknowledged uncertainty.

James Tour: 79 · Sy Garte: 80. Scores assess the reasoning in this debate.
