About this topic

Broad cumulative cases for or against God's existence and the reasonableness of belief, spanning several questions without one specialist issue dominating. Creator-only arguments are not treated as sufficient to establish a personal, involved God.

Debates are grouped by their main question. Scores assess the reasoning presented, not the truth of a position. Read the assessment method.
