# Explicitly authorized new team debate

Use this mode only for a new recording organized as two opposing teams with stable, source-confirmed membership. It overrides the dyadic-only eligibility restriction for that authorized run, not for historical reassessment or a panel. Read the repository's `docs/assessment-standalone-team-debate-v1.md` and `docs/assessment-multi-speaker-approximation-workflow-v1.md` completely. If the new-record profile and its end-to-end validators are not implemented, complete that implementation before claiming the lane is publication-ready.

Retain the ordinary single-debate runbook except where the repository's team extension explicitly supplies the alternate inventory, judgment, scoring, attribution, and disclosure contracts. Keep the historical multi-speaker census unchanged. Use a dedicated standalone registry profile, never a false dyadic flag or a counterfeit campaign batch.

The team is the scoring unit; each argument retains its actual speaker. Select repeated inferences once, document excluded repetitions, and score a later teammate's move only for its distinct incremental contribution. Freeze these decisions before independent judgments and require an independent full-source inventory audit. Attribute fallacies and biases to the actual move and speaker, without guilt by association or an extra score penalty.

Verify every selected move against audio, including speaker handoffs and quoted wording. Use the existing approximation calculator once after the resolved ledger; publish the approximation disclosure, primary-pass score ranges, and sensitivity notice. Exclude team scores and diagnostic contribution scores from individual rankings and averages. Keep team appearances available on members' profiles.

For a new speaker-labeled transcription route, run a small representative pilot before dispatching all selected clips. Verify the service's current reference-sample duration limits (the observed `gpt-4o-transcribe-diarize` limit is 1.2–10 seconds). Preserve a rejected request and its original inputs before one targeted parameter repair. Estimate remaining cost from returned input/output usage and current official token prices; a generic transcription per-minute estimate can substantially understate verbose speaker-labeled output. Stop before more paid requests if the revised projection exceeds the authorized cap, and request a new explicit cumulative cap. A successful transcription response is still not a completed attribution or cross-talk audit.

All ordinary prose-depth, AI Contribution, tag-review, repair, source-hash, cost, rendering, preflight, pull-request, merge, and deployment gates remain mandatory. An authorized team recording is not permission to relax those gates or claim dyadic calibration. Stop on unresolved source attribution, invalid team membership, a failed required gate, or cost beyond the authorized limit, preserving completed work.
