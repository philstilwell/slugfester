## Executive summary

**Religion, culture, and meaning still has the largest observed average gap, but a settled league table of topics would overstate the evidence.** Its 25 comparisons average 7.28 points in the skeptical direction. Resurrection and historical evidence is closest, at 3.04 points across 24 comparisons. Both figures are smaller than the September edition's 8.27 and 3.53.

The expanded set contains {{counts.religious}} comparisons across eight stable research groups. They are intentionally different from the website's more detailed browsing categories. Keeping the research groups stable makes the earlier and current editions comparable; it does not mean that creator-only arguments, personal theism, and religion's social effects are interchangeable.

Support-score differences remain in both constructive cases and replies. The practical lesson is to inspect the transition a speaker needs to defend: from usefulness to truth, from cause to agency, from testimony to a miracle, or from possible divine reasons to an explanation of observed suffering. The topic average is a starting point for that reading, not a verdict on the subject.

## 1. Preserve a useful comparison without freezing the website

The site now offers more topic distinctions than the September research did. Replacing the research categories silently would mix two changes: added evidence and a new classification scheme. This edition preserves eight exploratory groups, assigns each included assessment once, and publishes the decisions. The reader can therefore compare editions without mistaking a relabeling exercise for a change in performance.

“Primary topic” is a summary of the main burden, not a denial that other subjects arise. A broad God debate can discuss morality, cosmology, and evil. It belongs to General theism and naturalism here if no single specialized burden adequately describes the assessment. That group has grown to 62 comparisons; the other groups range from 21 to 27. Unequal group sizes affect how stable their averages are.

The research classification follows the position defended. It does not put every statement by a religious person into the religious side. Cases without an explicit enough religious-versus-skeptical contrast are excluded, while broad historical defenses receive narrower-set exclusions where appropriate. The classification table records both choices.

## 3. Look at the burden, not just the category name

### Religion's value and religion's truth

Community, consolation, and a shared moral vocabulary can be relevant to a motion about religion's effects. They do not automatically establish a supernatural explanation. Conversely, showing a disputed supernatural premise does not alone establish that a social institution has no benefits. A useful critique identifies which conclusion is actually under discussion and prevents either side from trading it for an easier one.

The [Sacks–Dawkins discussion](https://slugfester.com/debate/sacks-dawkins-science-religion-2012/) makes this distinction concrete. Compatibility between science and religion, the interpretation of scripture, and the independent support for cosmic purpose are different questions. A coherent division of roles can answer a conflict claim without proving the larger religious picture. Readers should track which burden each reply addresses.

### A creator is not yet a complete theology

Origins arguments can attempt to establish an initial cause, an intelligent source, or a necessary foundation. A further step is needed to identify that source with a personal, morally good, interventionist God. The [Loke–Linford assessment](https://slugfester.com/debate/loke-linford-physical-reality-cause-beginning-2023/) examines causes, physical beginnings, timeless grounding, and agency. A criticism of one step need not settle the rest.

The [Meyer–Atkins assessment](https://slugfester.com/debate/meyer-atkins-intelligent-design-science-2010/) asks whether design is scientifically eligible as an explanation of biological origins. It does not establish every religious attribute of a designer. The research group includes it as a broad design-versus-naturalistic comparison and offers an exclusion check in paper one. Keeping this distinction visible respects the difference between creator arguments and fuller theism.

### A possible explanation is not yet the better explanation

For suffering and hiddenness, it is important to distinguish showing logical consistency from explaining how likely the observed world is under competing accounts. A possible divine reason can defeat a claim of strict contradiction without defeating an evidential argument. A skeptical account must likewise do more than restate an intuition about what a deity would do.

The [Rauser–Malpass discussion](https://slugfester.com/debate/rauser-malpass-evidential-problem-evil-2021/) concerns comparative evidence, background beliefs, and suffering. The central reading question is what makes the observations more expected under one account than another. A score gap cannot replace that comparison.

### Sincerity, reliability, and a miracle

Witnesses can be sincere yet mistaken, and a report can preserve a sincere belief without establishing its supernatural explanation. Conversely, identifying uncertainty in one source does not establish a complete rival history. The [Horn–Paulogia assessment](https://slugfester.com/debate/horn-paulogia-apostolic-sincerity-2023/) distinguishes evidence for named leaders from confidence about all the original witnesses. Its scope problem is not interchangeable with the truth of resurrection.

No such simple pattern appears. A reply has its own burden. Pointing out that an opponent has not proved everything may be relevant, but a speaker who advances a rival positive claim must still support it. A constructive case must also address credible alternatives. The data therefore favor examining the inferential work in both roles, rather than attributing the whole gap to having to speak first.

## 5. What should change in a reader's approach?

**Reason 1:** the topic differences remain descriptively useful but are not sharply ordered. **Reason 2:** new cases can alter the average without establishing historical improvement or decline. **Reason 3:** the recurring interpretive issue concerns the strength of the step between premise and conclusion, not merely the subject label.

**Therefore:** use the topic map as a guide to questions. In cultural debates, separate value from truth. In origins debates, distinguish cause, agency, and theology. In historical debates, distinguish sincerity, reliability, and the proposed event. In evil debates, distinguish possibility from comparative expectation.

**Therefore:** do not use this table to choose a topic winner in advance. A fair extension would review classifications without seeing the score gaps, publish reasonable alternatives, and compare matched opponents and burdens. The current categories are exploratory, not a scheme specified before any evidence was examined.
