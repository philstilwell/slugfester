## 2. Which positions belong in this comparison?

**Classify the argument, not the identity of the speaker.** The broad set includes defenses of religion, scripture, purpose, supernatural claims, and creator or design arguments facing skeptical challenges. The title retains the familiar phrase “theist sides,” but this is not a pure sample of arguments for a personal, interventionist God.

The distinction between a creator and a fuller religious conception matters. Intelligence as an origin explanation does not by itself establish omniscience, goodness, revelation, or a particular religion. The new Meyer–Atkins and Loke–Linford cases are assigned to origins and design, with their narrower burdens recorded. Removing both leaves {{p1.sensitivity.without_new_origin_cases.n}} comparisons and a {{p1.sensitivity.without_new_origin_cases.mean:2}}-point gap, essentially the same headline.

By contrast, secular moral realism, free will, a historical human Jesus, and consciousness-first metaphysics do not by themselves establish the required religious-versus-skeptical contrast. The new Copan–Rauser conquest debate and Wood–Ally resurrection debate are excluded from this position study because they compare religious interpretations rather than a clean religious-versus-nonreligious contrast. Their assessments remain in the overall archive and other applicable studies.

The Horn–Paulogia sincerity debate enters the broad historical set, but not the narrower truth-claim check: sincerity is distinct from resurrection. The narrower selection also removes cultural-benefit and specified historical or doctrinal comparisons. Its {{p1.sensitivity.narrow.n}} cases retain a {{p1.sensitivity.narrow.mean:2}}-point gap. Every decision is available in the classification table; reasonable disagreement about a boundary is a reason for a sensitivity check, not for concealing it.

## 3. What the average does and does not say

Religious-side scores average {{p1.means.theist:2}}; skeptical-side scores average {{p1.means.non:2}}. Subtract within each debate first, then average with one vote per debate. This prevents a long or densely annotated debate from outweighing a shorter one solely because it contains more assessed moves. The middle difference is {{p1.gap.median:0}} points.

The middle 95% of averages from 20,000 repeated draws of whole debates runs from {{p1.gap.ci.0:2}} to {{p1.gap.ci.1:2}} points. Resampling means drawing again from the existing records, with replacement. It checks sensitivity to the observed mixture. It cannot repair caption errors, mistaken scores, a selective catalogue, or systematic judging bias. Recurring speakers mean these are not fully independent observations.

> A 5.74-point performance difference is not a probability that a worldview is false, a percentage of arguments that are invalid, or a measure of anyone's intelligence. It is a difference between recorded assessments under a particular rubric.

## 5. Read the transitions, including the counterexamples

In [Jones–Schmid on contingency](https://slugfester.com/debate/jones-schmid-contingency-god-2022/), the central issue includes the move from a necessary foundation to a divine mind. Granting a foundation does not automatically grant mentality, uniqueness, goodness, or religious identity. A stronger case supplies reasons for each extra property and compares alternatives at that same level of specificity.

The new [Meyer–Atkins discussion](https://slugfester.com/debate/meyer-atkins-intelligent-design-science-2010/) is an important counterexample to a one-directional narrative. The published assessment gives the design side a seven-point advantage. Its critiques distinguish spontaneous order from functionally specified sequence, origins from subsequent evolution, and scientific eligibility from proof of a designer. That does not establish intelligent design as true. It shows why a skeptical response can score poorly when it fails to engage the particular inference being defended.

In [Rauser–Aron Ra on revelation](https://slugfester.com/debate/rauser-aron-ra-biblical-violence-2022/), readers should likewise inspect the actual conditional claims rather than assume that a religious defense must be the weaker side. Examples are illustrations, not additional statistical evidence. The linked classification and casebook files make the wider record inspectable, including the cases that run against the average.

## 6. What follows for the hypothesis?

**Reason 1:** the gap remains across the broad set, narrower truth claims, both assessment processes, and several exclusions. **Reason 2:** support and inferential work account for much of the recorded difference. **Reason 3:** new comparisons reduce the gap and add substantial counterexamples, requiring a less sweeping narrative than the earlier edition.

**Therefore:** the assessments support a bounded claim about publicly supplied reasons in this archive. They do not establish that personal faith causes the weakness. Topic selection, opponent difficulty, rubric design, judging preferences, and repeated speakers remain alternative explanations or contributing influences.

A fair next test would judge matched arguments independently, conceal identities where feasible, apply the same burdens to skeptical and religious conclusions, and inspect disagreements against full source context. Evidence that those checks remove the gap would weaken the proposed explanation. Merely adding more similarly selected records would not settle it.

**Therefore:** readers should use the pattern to ask better questions of an argument, not to pre-judge a speaker. What exactly is claimed? What makes it more likely than its rivals? Which objection was answered? Which would change the conclusion? Those questions remain useful on either side.
