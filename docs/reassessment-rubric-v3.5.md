# Slugfester Reassessment Rubric v3.5

Version 3.5 governs deterministic classification compilation only. It inherits the substantive classification anchors of v3.4 and the scoring anchors of v2.1, but it does not select or expose participant-performance scores.

## Atomic target disposition

Treat component contact, distinct-example status, scope, eligible contrary material, and diagnostics as semantic proposals. Derive their coupled target disposition mechanically.

- A contacted component, eligible contrary item, nondefault scope, or non-none defect necessarily bears on the locked target.
- Component contact excludes contrary-material credit.
- A distinct connected example remains separate from original-target contact unless another proposal establishes target contact.
- Material already inside the locked target is a boundary classification, not a connected example.

Partial answers receive only the component contacts expressly licensed by the words. Global assent distributes across components only when it expressly ranges over the complete proposition without immediate restriction or redirection.

## Evidence

Every active nondefault proposal needs exact evidence text that occurs exactly once in the source excerpt. The compiler computes offsets. `connectionEvidence` supports a distinct connected example; `boundaryEvidence` supports only an inside-target classification. Inactive evidence is non-authoritative and is emitted as null.

## Atomic diagnostics

Defect and consequence are one bundle. A non-none defect needs an explicit eligible cue. A positive consequence additionally needs a separate defect-linked clause stating what inferentially fails. The bundle must come from a single source during adjudication.

## Burden relevance

Burden adjustment remains `retained` absent explicit reassignment or replacement of who must establish what. Burden contact requires exact evidence bearing on an eligible listed bridge. Shared raw burden values are locked and cannot be changed by review consensus.

## Charity

Use the strongest interpretation licensed by the actual words, but do not invent a component, diagnostic cue, consequence clause, replacement demand, or burden bridge. Compiler implications preserve coherence; they do not create substantive credit beyond a positive proposal with qualifying evidence.

## Classification-only exclusion

No score, score band selection, critique, Overall Commentary, AI Extension, winner, or production assessment may appear in a v3.5 artifact.
