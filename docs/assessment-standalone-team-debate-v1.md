# New standalone team debates v1

## Scope and authority

This opt-in extension supports a new recording explicitly organized as two opposing teams. It does not reclassify the frozen dyadic campaign, alter the historical 16-record multi-speaker census, or authorize rescoring an existing debate. Panels, shifting coalitions, and a substantive third side remain outside this extension. Freeze the user's authorization, the exact motion, team membership, and complete substantive window before inventory work.

Use the existing [multi-speaker approximation method](assessment-multi-speaker-approximation-workflow-v1.md), its unchanged numerical calculator, independent inventory audit, two isolated 5.6 Sol/low judgments, dispute-only adjudication, complete selected-move audio verification, score stability, and sensitivity diagnostics. The historical checkpoint population is not a prerequisite to this separately authorized single-record run and must not be changed. All other source, isolation, repair, and publication gates remain applicable. Do not claim the calibration pedigree of the dyadic method.

## Team ownership and repetition

Freeze an allowlist of named members for each team. Each selected move belongs to its actual speaker and exactly one team; a shared side score is not an individual score. Keep one speaker per scored move and retain any teammate adoption as an evidence-backed link, never an automatic transfer of credit.

Before judgment, group repeated claims by the inference they contribute. Select a repeated inference once. A later teammate move may be selected only for an identified new premise, answer, qualification, or concession, and its judgment must assess that incremental contribution rather than awarding the original argument again. Preserve excluded repetitions with their source intervals, speaker, retained move ID, and rationale. The independent inventory auditor must check these decisions against the complete source. Do not remove a distinct argument merely because teammates reach the same conclusion.

Use 4–7 semantic sections and the multi-speaker contract's 12–40 selected moves. Retain ordinary publication capacity (up to three cards per side per section, or the prelocked, evidence-justified fourth-row rule). Compute side and speaker counts from moves, not supplied audit totals. A section must contain evidence from both teams; never manufacture participation by every teammate in every section.

## Attribution and source boundaries

Review the whole recording to establish the substantive window. Exclude post-debate host commentary, advertisements, and unrelated discussion explicitly, without hiding debate concessions or replies. Enumerate moderator interventions and audience questions; evaluate only arguments the teams actually advance or adopt. A host's new substantive case cannot be silently treated as a team's argument.

Every selected move requires audio confirmation of speaker, beginning/end handoffs, cross-talk, and quote-eligible wording before the final ledger. Captions, a voice transcription without reliable speaker identification, and a model's assertion of confidence alone do not satisfy this gate. Preserve source and clip hashes and the actual verification method. If attribution or material wording cannot be verified within authorized access and cost, stop with the evidence intact.

## Publication and routing

Reserve the next available number in the standalone registry, using a separately validated `team-approximation-v1` profile. Never set `dyadicGatePassed` to true for a team recording. A team entry may not be marked published until a dedicated audit authenticates every stage and the ordinary production validator accepts its actual speaker ownership, team membership, quotations, prose, tags, and collective totals.

Use the exact rubric label `Slugfester Reassessment Rubric v2 — Multi-Speaker Approximation`. Show team totals, primary-pass ranges, and any format-sensitivity notice. Argument cards and fallacy/bias contexts name the responsible speaker. Include team appearances on all members' profiles but exclude the record from individual averages, rankings, opponent records, and score distributions. Do not publish diagnostic speaker contribution scores as individual ratings.

Retain the add-debate runbook's two independent post-score fallacy/bias reviews and full-union adjudication, natural AI Contribution prose, independent 25-debate content-depth comparison, desktop/mobile verification, `npm run site:preflight`, protected-branch pull request, required green check, merge, and deployment verification. Team authorization does not loosen word limits, alter scores after calculation, or change historical evidence. A sensitivity-induced change in the leading team requires review; it never authorizes a replacement score pass.
