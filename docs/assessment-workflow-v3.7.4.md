# Slugfester Disjoint Retired Atomic-Bundle Workflow v3.7.4

Version 3.7.4 tests the v3.7.3 atomic-choice method on twelve retired bundles whose case IDs and bundle IDs do not occur in the v3.7.3 correction smoke. The retired source corpus contains only three debates, so debate containers are reused and that limitation is explicit; no claim of debate-level holdout is permitted.

Two isolated 5.6 Sol contexts judge four anonymous complete bundles per debate. Candidate positions are counterbalanced and origins are sealed. Complete semantic tuples are compared only after both initial passes close. A third isolated context may receive only disputed bundles, and each final tuple requires two matching votes.

The test covers target-component contact, diagnostic classification and linkage, negative reframe defaults, and burden relevance. Target contrary disposition is derived mechanically. All candidate tuples are prevalidated. There is one attempt per context, no retry or repair, and no score or assessment prose may be derived.

A pass establishes repeatability on this disjoint retired-case sample only. It may authorize design of a larger retired workflow gate; it cannot correct the retired key, open held-out material, score participants, generate debate assessments, or mutate production content.
