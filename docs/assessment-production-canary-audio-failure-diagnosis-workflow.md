# Slugfester production-canary audio-failure diagnosis workflow

Preserve the four-move deterministic audio-verification result as an unresolved gate. Diagnose only whether an unresolved move is a request failure, missing evidence, insufficient expected-speaker recovery, insufficient expected-speaker duration, or a mixed-speaker lexical collision in which the full clip and expected-speaker recovery pass but the frozen recall-margin check fails.

Do not relax a threshold, rerun transcription, relabel a diarized segment, change a source span or proposition, or manually declare an attribution verified. A mixed-speaker lexical collision may authorize preparation of one isolated audio-attribution adjudication packet containing only the locked identity, expected speaker, proposition, failed deterministic evidence, and immutable raw diarized transcript.

Any future audio-attribution adjudicator must use 5.6 Sol with low reasoning effort through the ChatGPT subscription, receive no ratings, scores, legacy material, other debates, or publication prose, and decide only whether the expected speaker authored the core proposition. One attempt, no retry, and no paid transcription are permitted. An unresolved decision keeps all downstream adjudication and scoring blocked.
