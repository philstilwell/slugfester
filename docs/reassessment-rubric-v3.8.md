# Slugfester Burden-Contact Integration Rubric v3.8

Judge one complete anonymous composite candidate at a time.

## Exact proposition contact

1. Identify the proposition actually asserted, denied, or undercut by the move in its full context.
2. Route contact exists only when that proposition supplies a reason for or against one stated bridge, or directly attacks a necessary premise or inference of that bridge.
3. Shared subject matter, vocabulary, speaker side, responsiveness, target relevance, rhetorical opposition, or a merely possible downstream implication is insufficient.
4. Apply the compatibility check: if the move's proposition can remain true without making the bridge more or less credible, select no route contact.
5. A criticism of conduct performed or justified in the name of a doctrine does not contact the doctrine's truth or metaphysical grounding unless the move also challenges a necessary premise or inference of that bridge.

## Polarity

1. Select `support` when the move supplies a reason to accept the contacted bridge.
2. Select `attack` when it supplies a reason to reject, limit, or find insufficient the contacted bridge or a necessary inference within it.
3. The speaker's side and apparent intent are contextual evidence, not substitutes for expressed inferential force.

## Bridge selection

1. Select the narrowest stated bridge whose proposition receives exact contact.
2. A direct attack belongs to the bridge attacked, even when the response also suggests a different positive view.
3. Select a subsidiary bridge only when its specific proposition—not a generic narrower consideration—is expressed or attacked.
4. Select a central bridge only when the move contacts that stated central inference rather than one of its supporting examples.
5. Select the motion bridge only when the move states, supports, or attacks the complete motion-level conclusion. Contact with one route premise does not inherit motion status.

## No-contact default

When no candidate bridge passes the exact proposition test, choose no route contact even if the move is important, responsive, damaging to an interlocutor, or relevant to the debate generally. Burden contact is narrower than argumentative quality or responsiveness.

Evidence must be one exact, case-sensitive substring occurring once in the atomic excerpt. The rationale must identify the move's proposition, apply the contact or no-contact rule, state polarity when applicable, name the selected bridge proposition, and exclude the nearest competitor.
