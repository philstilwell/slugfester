# Slugfester Deterministic Disagreement and Audio Preparation v4.2.21.17.3

The six accepted independent judgments are compared field by field without a model call. The locked inventory remains authoritative for routes, sections, chronology, candidate selection, proposition, source span, attribution, and move kind. Importance is not inventory-locked; it is a performance-judgment field and any A/B difference is sent to adjudication.

Response structure is disputed whenever the repository-derived class, legal targets, component contacts, or closed response findings differ. A within-class responsiveness difference greater than five points is disputed. Burden-contact, precision, and calibration finding differences expose their dependent scoring fields. Other scoring dimensions are disputed only when the A/B difference exceeds five points. Smaller scalar differences are retained for a deterministic rounded mean after adjudication. Rationale wording alone does not create a dispute.

Audio verification is mandatory when either independent pass assigns less than high assessment confidence or when the repository-owned attribution confidence is less than high. The audio work item is derived from the locked source span and locally stored timestamped transcript events. Audio must be verified before any adjudication packet containing that move is released.

No aggregate, move, section, side, or debate score is calculated during extraction. The next authorized action is local audio-source preparation only.
