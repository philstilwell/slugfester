## Result

## Fallacy and cognitive-bias review

Every locked move was checked against all 13 local LogFall and CogBias definitions through two fresh blind passes—546 definition comparisons per pass. Each label identifies the same material weakness already expressed in its locked critique. No label altered a judgment, move, score, critique, or winner.
