## Fallacy and cognitive-bias review

Every locked move was checked against all 13 local LogFall and CogBias definitions through two fresh blind passes, totaling 364 definition comparisons per pass. A separate adjudicator reviewed the anonymous union of 30 serious candidates, and a conservative final checker retested every decision against the complete source and locked critique.

Four labels survived, all fallacies. **Equivocation** applies where Wolpe shifts belief from factual assent about God to an existential orientation. **Begging the question** applies where the asserted transcendent moral order is used to ground the very moral order needing support. **Special pleading** applies where classifying God and souls as nonphysical is used to exempt their alleged effects from evidential testing. A second **Equivocation** applies where experiential or metaphorical truth is allowed to stand in for truth about a Christian interpretation.

Twenty-six candidates were rejected because ordinary underargument, incomplete support, broad comparison, weak analogy, or speculative psychology did not satisfy the exact catalog definition. Fourteen of those rejected candidates were proposed cognitive biases; none survived because the excerpts did not establish the required selective weighting, base-rate failure, scope failure, or subjective-validation pattern. No quota was used, and no label altered a judgment, move, critique, score, or result.
