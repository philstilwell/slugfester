Assessment based on complete public English automatic captions for the World Video Bible School recording of Kyle Butt and Dan Barker at the University of South Carolina on February 12, 2009. The debate-only portion of Kyle Butt’s published transcript was used to corroborate selected wording and speaker turns; its editorial commentary was excluded. This was textual verification, not direct listening. Known caption errors are restricted and were not treated as speaker mistakes. Direct quotes retain eligible caption wording; argument cards condense the speakers’ reasoning. This assessment covers the formal opening statements, rebuttals, and cross-examination (approximately 5:54–1:08:57), plus both closing statements (1:46:09–1:57:06). The entire audience Q&A, including both speakers’ answers and dependent replies, is excluded at the editor’s request. Neither speaker receives credit or penalty for excluded material. These scores assess the formal rounds and closings, not the complete event. Approximately 74 minutes of substantive formal material are eligible within the 1 hour 58 minute recording.
