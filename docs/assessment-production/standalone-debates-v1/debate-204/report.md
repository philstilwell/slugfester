# Debate 204 production report

Status: **published and frozen**.

## Result

Dinesh D'Souza and Peter Singer debate whether morality can exist without God. D'Souza defends the claim that morality requires God; Singer argues that secular moral reasons are sufficient. The one permitted repository calculation scored D'Souza **71** and Singer **81**, so Singer prevailed by ten points.

The result was stable across the two isolated judgments: their mean absolute distance was 0.75 points, the largest difference was one point, neither final score moved outside the judgments' range, and both judgments agreed on the winner.
