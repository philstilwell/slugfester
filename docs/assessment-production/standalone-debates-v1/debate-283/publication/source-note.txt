Based on complete English automatic captions for the 2009 D’Souza–Barker debate, with coverage checked against captions from the organizer’s two-part recording. Automated transcription of seven source-audio clips clarified speaker changes and uncertain wording; no direct listening is claimed. Moderator and audience questions provide context, while only the two debaters’ arguments and replies are assessed. Repeated passages are counted once, and garbled incidental names are not grounds for credit or deduction. Quotations preserve exact caption wording; argument descriptions are condensed summaries. Later qualifications are retained, including Barker’s distinction between a good God and God more generally, and D’Souza’s distinction between belief and direct knowledge.
