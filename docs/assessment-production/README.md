# Assessment-production archive

The SLUGFESTER reassessment campaign is complete. It published 174 reassessments: 10 checkpoint debates and every one of the 164 debates in the frozen continuation pool. A separate post-campaign release then promoted the five already-finalized dyadic calibration debates, bringing the production ledger total to 179 without new judgments or scoring. Batch 17 remains the final campaign batch, and no Batch 18 selection is permitted.

Start with these records:

- [Campaign closure report](campaign-closure-v1/report.md) — human-readable verified outcome.
- [Campaign closure manifest](campaign-closure-v1/manifest.json) — machine-readable identities, hashes, scores, costs, recovery counts, and evidence records.
- [Campaign closure validation summary](campaign-closure-v1/validation-summary.json) — commands and qualifications from the closure run.
- [Post-campaign operator guide](post-campaign-handoff-v1/operator-guide.md) — current validation commands, immutable boundaries, and safe site-maintenance procedure.
- [Post-campaign handoff report](post-campaign-handoff-v1/report.md) — maintainability changes and release-readiness results added after closure.
- [Calibration-promotion report](calibration-promotion-v1/report.md) — exact publication record for Debates 51, 63, 90, 153, and 165.
- [Calibration-promotion manifest](calibration-promotion-v1/manifest.json) — frozen source, candidate, score, route, and ledger locks for the supplemental release.
- [Calibration-promotion validation summary](calibration-promotion-v1/validation-summary.json) — fresh production rendering and repository-validation results.

The many other directories under this archive are retained execution evidence. Their failures, recovery attempts, judgments, ledgers, audio checks, rendering records, and publication locks are historical records, not templates to rewrite or clean up.
