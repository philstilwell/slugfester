# Featured quotations

A featured quotation must express a complete thought when read without its supporting context. Select eligible complete excerpts before freezing the inventory, then review the final choice against adjacent source sentences before publication. Preserve any condition, qualification, or immediate challenge needed to represent the speaker fairly. A concession followed by a challenge should not be displayed as an unqualified endorsement.

Keep the current 3–18-word limit by choosing another complete source excerpt when necessary. Never invent words, silently paraphrase inside quotation marks, or stop before a sentence’s object or conclusion. Supporting text may identify a referent and explain the setting; it must not supply the quotation’s missing argument. Completeness is an editorial judgment: word counts, terminal punctuation, and substring checks alone cannot establish it.

For a user-authorized correction to an already frozen standalone assessment, preserve the original publication, inventory, scores, and ledger. Register a hash-verified `featuredQuoteCorrection` supplement that names the original publication and full source inventory, records the original and replacement strings, and restricts each shard to at most two fields. Only `quotes.pro.text`, `quotes.pro.context`, `quotes.con.text`, and `quotes.con.context` may change. Revised quotation words must match an exact excerpt of the same speaker’s locked full source span; separately record any capitalization or punctuation normalization and a source-specific completeness review. The supplement does not retroactively change the original quote-eligible selections or any judgment.

The production audit and browser checks reconstruct the expected quotation revision from that supplement. The content-parity audit uses a separately preserved revised report. All other publication fields and all historical evidence remain subject to their original checks. Reject corrections that require new evidence, invented wording, or altered assessment substance through this route.
