# Slugfester Inventory Transport Recovery v4.2.21.16.2

The recovery protocol projects the complete candidate-evidence bundle into an inventory-specific model transport. It retains every candidate in the original order and retains every field needed for score-blind semantic selection: candidate ID, side, speaker, source chronology, discovery move-kind advisory, proposed proposition, load-bearing level and reason, response intent, context summary, and source-exact excerpt.

The model transport omits attribution confidence, attribution basis, candidate confidence, and evidence-rendering diagnostics. Those fields are not selection inputs; they remain hash-locked in the complete evidence bundle used by deterministic validation and are restored by the repository after selection. No candidate, proposition, reason, context summary, or source excerpt is removed or shortened. Semantic candidate downselection remains prohibited.

The prior gate's accepted Debate 133 and 178 locked inventories remain unchanged. Only Debate 182 receives one fresh attempt under this successor protocol. The attempt remains score-blind and cannot emit response topology, ratings, scores, winner labels, Overall Commentary, AI Extension, or publication prose.

The recovery passes only if Debate 182 produces a valid locked inventory with source-exact final evidence, after which all three accepted inventories must validate together by frozen hashes. Independent judgment preparation remains prohibited until that combined 3/3 analysis passes.
