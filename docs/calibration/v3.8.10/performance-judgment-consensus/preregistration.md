# v3.8.10 retired three-debate clean scoring gate

The frozen population is retired Debates 55, 103, and 161. Hash-locked source, coverage, section, burden-contact, and completed audio-verification records may be inherited from v3.8.8. No v3.8.8 or v3.8.9 performance judgments, derived scores, assessment prose, novelty decisions, or adversarial conclusions may be supplied to a model.

The execution uses 5.6 Sol through the ChatGPT subscription: two fresh isolated passes per debate, deterministic disagreement extraction, and one fresh isolated adjudicator per debate receiving only disputed fields and anonymous candidate values. Scores are derived only after the final consensus ledger validates.

Before any real debate context, one synthetic context must prove endpoint acceptance and packet-aware validity of the exact shared scoring schema across every response class, both charity states, and the zero-adjustment exclusion path. Deterministic validation may not use lexical tests on free-text rationales. Each real context has one attempt, no automatic retry, and no representation or semantic recovery. Any invalid real context fails the gate.

Material scalar disagreements are deltas greater than five; a diagnostic move-total delta greater than four exposes all unequal ratings for that move. Response-tuple and charity-tested mismatches are always disputed. Nondisputed unequal scalars merge by rounded mean. The adjudicator chooses only candidate 1 or 2 and may not invent a third value.

The formal scalar-dispute ceiling is 0.25. A rate above 0.23 is a blocking warning for held-out authorization even if the formal ceiling passes. Maximum diagnostic overall delta is five, diagnostic winner labels must match across both passes and final scores, and Spearman correlation across the six side totals must be at least 0.90.

Metered model API cost is capped at $0. New transcription is allowed only for required audio verification and capped at $0.12 across the gate. Production debate data and rankings remain immutable.
