# v4.2.3 source-only selection assessment

The v4.2.3 sample was frozen correctly but rejected during source-only motion screening before packet preparation or model execution.

Debate 116 concerns pain, pleasure, horrendous evil, soul-building, skeptical theism, and whether evil favors naturalism. The selector assigned it to mind/agency because that family's earlier regex included the bare token `soul`, which matched `soul-building`. Substantively it belongs to evil/hiddenness. Because Debate 157 also belongs to evil/hiddenness, the tuple has two debates from that family and no genuine mind/agency debate.

This is a deterministic selection defect, not a judgment or transport failure. No transcript content beyond the already permitted source-only motion metadata was used. No packet, model context, score, winner, legacy comparator, or publication artifact was created.

All six revealed v4.2.3 identities are excluded from the replacement fresh gate. The replacement classifier must test evil/hiddenness before mind/agency and remove bare `soul` from the latter while retaining explicit consciousness, mind, free-will, personal-identity, agency, and mental-causation terms.
