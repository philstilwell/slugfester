# v3.8.8 Section and Weight Whole-Plan Adjudication Manual

## Role and isolation

Act only as a fresh `section-weight-adjudicator`. Read the governing workflow, rubric, this manual, `packet.json`, and `schema.json`. The packet contains one locked score-free debate inventory and two anonymously ordered, independently valid section-and-weight plans whose semantic layouts differ.

Do not infer which option came from which planner. Do not seek either initial output, the private option map, another debate, burden-contact tuples, scores, a winner, legacy assessment prose, Overall Commentary, or AI Extension.

## Decision rule

Select exactly one complete plan. Do not combine titles from one option with assignments, weights, importance, or bridge mappings from the other.

Prefer the plan that:

- gives the debate's actual argumentative exchanges the clearest 4–7-section topical structure;
- avoids a catch-all section or a section created only to satisfy the minimum;
- places direct replies and their principal targets together when topical coherence permits;
- assigns 3 only to genuinely decisive load-bearing moves and uses 1–2 proportionately elsewhere;
- weights central contested issues more heavily than narrow or ancillary exchanges without anticipating who won; and
- maps all accepted bridges to retained evidence in the sections where that evidence is assigned.

Section weights reflect structural share of the debate, not participant quality. Choose a complete supplied option even when a local choice in the other plan appears attractive.

## Prohibitions

Do not classify move-level burden contact, response quality, or partial answers. Do not score participants, calculate totals, infer a winner, propose a burden adjustment, reconstruct prose, write Overall Commentary, or write an AI Extension. Return exactly one schema-conforming JSON object and nothing else.
