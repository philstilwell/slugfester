## Design improvement

The packet representation no longer forces every excerpt onto a route bridge. It includes an explicit no-contact candidate, encodes support or attack directly, and binds polarity to one exact bridge in a valid composite. Generic subsidiary descriptions were replaced with proposition-bearing sub-bridges for the affected routes. This prevents a vague “narrower consideration” option from absorbing topical but non-propositional overlap.

The rubric makes exact proposition contact the first decision. Speaker side and responsiveness are context, not proof of contact; motion status requires the complete conclusion; and a direct rebuttal is attached to the bridge actually attacked.

## Limits and next step

The cases are exposed development material, and the sealed design fixtures are provisional AI-authored expectations used only to test candidate coverage. They are neither human ground truth nor a benchmark. The enlarged seven-to-nine-option composite universe may itself create cognitive load, so fresh-model repeatability remains unknown.

Packet engineering and conceptual decomposition are at an **A level**; semantic reliability is **not yet graded**. The next justified step is a separately frozen execution layer with a synthetic dispute-only adjudication test. Model execution must remain blocked until that runner, extractor, analyzer, and strict 8-of-8 threshold pass an independent lock.
