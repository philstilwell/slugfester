## What improved

The composite representation removed the instability observed in v3.7.5. Each reviewer had to choose one internally valid state: no route contact, support for one exact bridge, or attack on one exact bridge. This prevented incompatible contact, polarity, and bridge values from being assembled independently.

The proposition-contact rule, explicit no-contact option, narrowest-bridge rule, and complete-motion exclusion were all repeatable on this exposed set. The two passes agreed across all observed categories: no contact, support, attack, central, subsidiary, and motion. They also agreed on the formerly disputed `burden-contact-185-05`, selecting support for the subsidiary indeterminacy bridge in both counterbalanced packets.

## Stable differences from provisional fixtures

The two fixture differences are diagnostics, not gate failures. The fixtures are sealed AI-authored development expectations rather than human ground truth, and the preregistered threshold measures independent semantic agreement.

`burden-contact-62-04` was classified by both passes as support for the subsidiary social-unity bridge rather than support for the provisional central bridge. The excerpt specifically describes people submitting to a shared idea of what they will do. The subsidiary bridge expresses that exact collective-coordination proposition; the central bridge makes the broader claim that patterns and values are useful mind-grounded abstractions. The stable narrower classification is well supported by the v3.7.6 rule.

`burden-contact-185-05` was classified by both passes as support for the subsidiary indeterminacy bridge rather than attack on the provisional central sourcehood bridge. The excerpt says libertarian free will would neither confer nor remove responsibility. That directly supports the narrower claim that an indeterministic or libertarian addition does not rescue basic-desert control. It does not state the central bridge's luck-to-sourcehood premise. The composite representation therefore appears to have resolved the v3.7.5 split without promoting the move to a broader bridge.

Neither stable difference authorizes rewriting a benchmark. They should remain recorded as evidence that provisional AI fixtures can be broader or differently polarized than repeatable proposition-level readings.

## Quality judgment and next step

Engineering, isolation, provenance, schema enforcement, and cost control are at an **A level**. Composite burden-contact repeatability is also **A-level within this narrow exposed correction smoke**. The result is materially stronger than v3.7.5 because it achieved perfect initial agreement while preserving exact semantic decoding under option counterbalancing.

The evidence is not yet sufficient for the 195-debate batch. These are eight exposed development cases from three debates, every speaker attribution is high confidence, no real dispute exercised pass C, and no numerical scores or assessment prose were produced. The synthetic fixture verifies dispute-only adjudication mechanics but cannot substitute for a naturally occurring model disagreement.

The next justified step is a separately frozen, case-disjoint retired three-debate burden-contact test. Its cases should be absent from v3.7.6 development and should deliberately cover no contact, both polarities, all three bridge tiers, and close central-versus-subsidiary and motion-versus-route competitors. It should retain two isolated initial passes, deterministic semantic disagreement extraction, a third pass receiving only disputed cases, two-vote final resolution, audio verification for every medium-confidence attribution, and scores derived only after adjudication. Thresholds and artifacts must be committed before inference. A pass would support testing the corrected representation in a larger held-out workflow; it would still not by itself authorize production reassessment of all 195 debates.
