# v3.8.3 held-out classification-packet assessment

## Outcome

Deterministic packet construction passed. The 12 locked moves from v3.8.2 produced six classification packets: one packet for each of two independent passes across Debates 103, 55, and 161. Every packet contains four moves and the complete 21-state burden-contact universe. No model context was executed during construction.

All 12 atomic excerpts matched exactly one span in their hash-verified local timestamped events, and all recorded event and time coordinates reproduced. Every selected attribution is high confidence, so the mandatory audio policy produces zero required or pending verifications rather than bypassing an existing requirement.

## Independence and leakage audit

The model-visible packets exclude provisional burden contacts, source-selection warnings, source proposition summaries, attribution rationales, reviewer outputs, and adjudication rationales. The sealed option map is not model-visible. The packets retain only the evidence and structure needed for independent classification: atomic excerpt, source coordinates, speaker and side, motion, route maps, and anonymous composite candidates.

For every move, pass A and pass B contain identical semantic candidate sets but assign every tuple to a different option position. The universe includes no contact plus support and attack for all ten bridges on both routes. This avoids the single-route restriction of the retired test and permits the expressed proposition—not the speaker's side—to determine polarity and target.

Packet engineering and source integrity are at an **A level**. The construction is ready for a separately hash-frozen classification execution using two isolated 5.6 Sol passes per debate and dispute-only adjudication. This assessment authorizes that preregistration only. It does not authorize scoring, participant assessments, Overall Commentary, AI Extension, benchmark changes, production changes, or rollout to all debates.
