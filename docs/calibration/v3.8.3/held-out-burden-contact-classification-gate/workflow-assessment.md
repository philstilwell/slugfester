## Independence evidence

Neither pass could access the sealed option map, source-preparation contact, selection warning, another classifier output, gate threshold, prior result, legacy assessment, or assessment prose. Both passes received only the same invariant rules, locked packet, full local transcript, and timestamped events.

The strongest evidence that this separation was real is `heldout-contact-161-07`. The source-stage provisional aid placed the move at the con motion bridge. Both independent classifiers instead selected support for the narrower con subsidiary-3 bridge concerning evil and opaque divine presence. Their rationales applied the frozen specificity rule: the expressed proposition concerns what evil makes expected under a perfect God; the broader skeptical conclusion is downstream. The provisional value was never a truth key, so this 11-of-12 diagnostic match is informative rather than a gate failure.

Isolation, provenance, schema enforcement, option counterbalancing, and deterministic decoding performed at an **A level**. The frozen dispute path was not invoked, but its dry fixture separately verified no-contact, polarity, and bridge/tier disputes; two-value-only adjudication; and two-vote resolution.

Composite burden-contact classification is **A within this three-debate held-out gate**. Perfect repeatability, full category coverage, and the independent correction of one provisional selection aid support advancing the workflow. This grade measures repeatability under the locked AI rubric, not human truth.

## Remaining limits

The sample contains only three dyadic debates and 12 selected moves. It does not establish behavior across the full corpus, debates with three or more substantive speakers, or weak and ambiguous moves outside the selected inventory. All attributions were high confidence, so the mandatory audio-verification branch remained untriggered in live execution.

This gate did not derive numerical participant scores, evaluate the full set of rubric dimensions, generate participant assessments, write Overall Commentary or AI Extension, or mutate a production debate. Perfect burden-contact agreement therefore does not by itself make the full reassessment workflow ready for all 195 debates.

## Readiness judgment and next step

The adjudicated-consensus workflow is ready for a separately preregistered three-debate score-derivation and assessment-reconstruction gate using these 12 locked classifications as its burden-contact ledger. The next design must enforce the single scoring-pass schema, derive scores only from completely resolved semantic fields, preserve the strengthened burden-adjustment exclusion rule, and keep two independent Sol judgments plus dispute-only adjudication for any remaining non-deterministic assessment fields.

Assessment prose must be generated only after the score ledger is locked. That final reconstruction should include participant assessments, Overall Commentary, and the clearly labeled AI Extension accordion, while retaining the requested visual distinction and avoiding the prohibited wording. A successful end-to-end gate would justify a larger held-out consistency test; it would still require an explicit rollout decision before all 195 debates.
