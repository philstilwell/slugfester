# v4.1.7 fresh-six workflow failure assessment

The v4.1.7 gate is not ready for score derivation or legacy comparison. Its compute path passed: all six source-only primary contexts and all five triggered Pass B contexts were valid on their only attempts, and the updated 195-debate projection remained 40.73 central / 54.64 conservative hours. The audio branch also retained all four implicated source files and 12 diarized transcripts locally, completing 12 one-attempt calls at an estimated $0.2289.

The quality gate nevertheless failed for a substantive provenance reason. Debate 91's locked `con-apriori-reply` claims that Graham Oppy said pain, unlike pleasure, causes and rationalizes aversive behavior, but the declared 32:49–34:52 source span contains a different exchange about identity theory and the epistemic gap. The quoted pain proposition occurs elsewhere in the debate. Deterministic audio checks failed, and the isolated Sol/high attribution adjudicator independently concluded that Oppy's supplied segments do not express the locked proposition. No score or legacy comparator was opened.

A corpus-wide check of the six fresh outputs found nine of 60 moves with at least one source-span integrity failure: two excerpt/span recall failures and seven event/end-time inconsistencies under a 250-millisecond tolerance. The existing primary validator accepted them because it verifies only numeric index and duration bounds; it does not load the event text to verify that the excerpt belongs to the declared span or that the declared end belongs to the declared end event.

The next workflow version should fail primary output validation unless the declared start time equals the start event, the declared end time falls inside the end event within a small fixed tolerance, and the excerpt reaches a frozen ordered/lexical coverage threshold against only the declared events. The validator must receive and hash the complete event file. The correction should be fixture-tested with wrong-span, truncated-span, and wrong-excerpt mutations.

Debate 91 and every artifact derived from its invalid locked inventory must remain diagnostic only. The recommended next external test is a new prospective source-blind sample under the corrected validator, not a post-hoc edit of v4.1.7 and not a score patch. The other five debates are useful engineering evidence but do not rescue this gate. Until the corrected fresh gate passes, disagreement extraction, adjudicated score derivation, legacy comparison, the held-out gate, and the 195-debate reassessment remain unauthorized.
