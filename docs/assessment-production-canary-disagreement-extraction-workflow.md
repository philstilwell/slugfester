# Slugfester production-canary deterministic disagreement extraction workflow

## Scope

Compare the twenty accepted independent judgments for Debates 05, 13, 37, 64, 65, 81, 130, 138, 152, and 188 field by field without a model or audio call. Pass A and Pass B remain isolated historical inputs. The score-blind inventory remains authoritative for routes, sections, chronology, candidate selection, proposition, source span, attribution, and move kind. Importance is a performance-judgment field, so any A/B importance difference is disputed.

## Dispute contract

Response structure is disputed whenever the repository-derived class, legal targets, component contacts, or closed response findings differ. A within-class responsiveness difference greater than five points is disputed. Burden-contact, precision, and calibration finding differences expose their dependent scoring fields. Other scoring dimensions are disputed only when the A/B difference exceeds five points. Smaller scalar differences are retained for a deterministic rounded mean after adjudication. Rationale wording alone does not create a dispute.

## Audio queue

Audio verification is mandatory when either independent pass assigns less than high assessment confidence or when the repository-owned attribution confidence is less than high. Every work item must be derived from the locked source span and the unchanged local timestamped events. The extracted queue must exactly reproduce the four-move confidence queue frozen by the independent-judgment gate. No queued move may enter an adjudication packet before audio verification passes.

## Stop boundary

Do not calculate aggregate, move, section, side, or debate scores during extraction. A passing extraction authorizes local audio-source preparation only. It does not authorize paid transcription, audio-verification execution, adjudication-packet preparation, adjudication-model execution, final-ledger assembly, score derivation, publication finalization, production mutation, or any remaining production batch.
