# Slugfester Reassessment Rubric v3.8.11

Judge only the argumentative performance in the locked full transcript. Do not score a worldview's truth, popularity, moral appeal, pedigree, speaking style, accent, speed, status, confidence of delivery, or audience reaction. Structural classifications constrain scores but do not themselves guarantee quality.

## Shared anchors

| Band | Operational meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 95–100 | Exceptional and unusually complete; the inferential route, warrant, live engagement, and qualifications require little material repair. |
| 85–94 | Very strong; the move substantially advances its burden and survives the strongest live objection with only bounded residual gaps. |
| 75–84 | Strong/competent; coherent and relevant, but one important warrant, bridge, or qualification remains compressed. |
| 65–74 | Mixed; a useful core remains materially underdeveloped. |
| 50–64 | Weak; a central burden or live issue is insufficiently discharged despite some relevant content. |
| 25–49 | Very weak; the inference, support, or engagement is seriously defective. |
| 0–24 | Non-performance on the assigned issue or no recoverable argumentative route. |

Use 50, 70, 80, and 90 as boundaries, not default magnets. A difference of five or more points requires a named source feature or missing bridge.

## Logical coherence — 25%

- **90–100:** explicit and mutually consistent premises, intermediate steps, scope, and conclusion; the strongest live validity challenge is resolved.
- **80–89:** a stable inferential route with one bounded implicit bridge that does not threaten the conclusion.
- **70–79:** an intelligible route with one material bridge, scope condition, or ambiguity unresolved.
- **50–69:** a substantial equivocation, unsupported conditional, false dilemma, internal tension, or non sequitur weakens the route.
- **0–49:** self-undermining, plainly invalid at its core, or no usable route to the conclusion.

Evidence weakness belongs primarily under evidence/warrant unless it creates a distinct inferential failure.

## Evidence and warrant — 20%

- **90–100:** specific, sourceable, representative evidence, defended warrants, and serious comparison with live rival explanations.
- **80–89:** strong support with one bounded documentation, independence, base-rate, or rival-hypothesis gap.
- **70–79:** appropriate support exists, but a material warrant or verification step is compressed.
- **50–69:** assertion, anecdote, authority, selective examples, or speculative mechanisms carry substantial weight.
- **0–49:** a fact-dependent conclusion is effectively unsupported or contradicted within the locked source.

An absent inline citation is not itself a deduction. Judge how much verification the inference needs and how much the debate supplies.

## Responsiveness — 20%

Classify the response before selecting the number. Coverage concerns what the move contacts; coherence and evidence concern whether the answer succeeds.

### Constructive opening

- **90–100:** states or completes a motion-level route and its success conditions.
- **80–89:** advances the central route with one bounded component implicit.
- **70–79:** directly establishes a necessary subsidiary burden.
- **40–69:** topical case-building that is peripheral or only loosely connected to the adopted burden.
- **0–39:** off-motion, burden-substituting, or not a usable constructive contribution.

### Replies

| Class | Exact test | Range |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Full answer | Contacts every indispensable component of the strongest live target. | 80–100 |
| Diagnostic defeat | Expressly identifies a defect and separately states a consequence that, if correct, defeats the target. | 80–100 |
| Justified reframe | Explains why the governing demand is malformed and answers a stated replacement demand. | 80–100 |
| Partial answer | Contacts at least one indispensable target component and leaves at least one other indispensable component untouched. | 55–79 |
| Relevant nonanswer | Supplies issue-bearing contrary material without contacting an indispensable component. | 40–69 |
| Nonanswer | Neither contacts an indispensable component nor supplies issue-bearing contrary material; includes materially weaker substitution. | 0–39 |

Within `full answer`, use 90+ only for explicit engagement with the decisive premise or inference; use 80–89 for complete but compressed or indirect coverage. Within `partial answer`, use 70–79 when the decisive component is contacted and the omission is bounded, and 55–69 when only a secondary indispensable component is contacted. Within `relevant nonanswer`, use 60–69 for substantial pressure and 40–59 for real but peripheral contact.

A compound target may contain only independently necessary components. An ornamental clause cannot manufacture partiality. Contact does not propagate through logical dependency. A nearby counterargument, weaker reconstruction, demand change, or later answer is not a partial answer unless the selected move itself contacts a locked indispensable component.

A partial answer is not automatically incoherent, irrelevant, or uncharitable. Apply a second effect only when the source shows a distinct inferential, burden, or representational consequence.

For auditability, a responsive judgment's rationale must name the indispensable target component actually contacted and, whenever coverage is incomplete, the indispensable component left unanswered. These descriptions are evidence, not additional semantic fields, and may not be normalized after output. A reply is judged only in its locked span; a later answer does not retroactively make the earlier move complete.

## Relevance and burden — 15%

Use the final adjudicated burden-contact tuple. Supporting and attacking an eligible bridge receive the same tier; polarity does not by itself establish quality.

| Final contact | Range | Operational test |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Motion bridge | 90–100 | States, supports, or attacks the complete motion-level conclusion proportionately. |
| Central bridge | 75–89 | Materially advances or defeats the route's central inferential bridge. |
| Subsidiary bridge | 55–74 | Advances or defeats one necessary subsidiary bridge without establishing the motion-level consequence. |
| No exact route contact | 0–54 | Topical but peripheral, directed at an unadopted burden, or irrelevant to the locked route. |

For no exact route contact, use 40–54 for a meaningful topical contribution outside the adopted route, 20–39 for a weak or burden-shifting connection, and 0–19 for irrelevance. A critic does not inherit a positive contrary burden merely by challenging a constructive case. Score only the selected speaker and span; do not import another participant's view or a later consequence.

The locked tier is a ceiling-and-floor band, not an automatic score. Within the band, rate how effectively the expressed span advances or attacks the named bridge. General topic relevance, a neighboring bridge, the importance of the speaker's worldview, or a later consequence cannot substitute for exact contact.

## Precision and clarity — 10%

- **90–100:** terms, modality, comparison class, scope, referents, and confidence are stable and auditable.
- **80–89:** clear with one bounded ambiguity that does not alter the inference.
- **70–79:** understandable but compressed or ambiguous at one material point.
- **50–69:** vague quantifiers, unstable terms, unclear referents, or missing distinctions materially obscure evaluation.
- **0–49:** the claim cannot be reconstructed reliably enough to assess.

Fluency, wit, polish, and technical density are not clarity unless they improve recoverability.

## Epistemic calibration — half of the final 10%

- **90–100:** confidence, modality, and qualifications closely match the supplied warrant; live uncertainty is explicit.
- **80–89:** proportionate with one minor overstatement or omitted caveat.
- **70–79:** one material step beyond the warrant or one necessary caveat is missing.
- **50–69:** substantial overclaiming, selective skepticism, or certainty unsupported by the selected span.
- **0–49:** certainty is radically disproportionate to the available warrant.

Advocacy confidence is not overconfidence when the warrant supports it. A cautious tone does not earn calibration when the inference remains overstated.

## Representational charity — half of the final 10%

- **90–100:** represents the strongest live alternative accurately, including its decisive qualification, before criticism.
- **80–89:** fair and materially accurate with one minor omission.
- **70–79:** charity is not materially tested because no live alternative is represented or attacked; use exactly **75** and state that it was not tested.
- **50–69:** a materially weaker reconstruction, omitted decisive qualification, or unsupported motive attribution affects the argument.
- **0–49:** the move depends on a gross caricature of the live position.

Politeness is not charity, and abrasiveness is not uncharitable unless it changes the argumentative representation. A partial answer can be charitable when it accurately states its limited scope. Do not infer intent, and do not repair or condemn the selected move with wording from elsewhere.

Set `charityTested` to true only if the move represents, attacks, or inferentially relies on a live alternative position. When false, use exactly 75 and explicitly state that charity was not tested. When true, identify the represented alternative and its decisive qualification; disagreement, compression that does not weaken the alternative, and failure to answer are not by themselves charity defects.

The calculator alone combines the subratings:

`calibration/charity = round((epistemic calibration + representational charity) / 2)`

## Burden-completion adjustment

Zero is the default. A nonzero value requires one explicit debate-wide consequence that changes a named locked success criterion and remains absent from every move rating, response link, omission record, importance value, section weight, and other adjustment. The record must identify affected burden IDs, related move IDs, the criterion, the distinct consequence, every tested duplicate location, and a counterfactual.

If any duplicate location contains the consequence, if `alreadyCapturedBy` is nonempty, if `notAlreadyScored` is false, if either pass identifies a materially different consequence, or if the concern is inventory coverage, style, repetition, cumulative impression, worldview plausibility, or dissatisfaction with weights, the final value is zero. A nonzero adjustment must name the exact locked success criterion, related move IDs, every duplicate-capture location tested, and a counterfactual material misstatement. The adjustment cannot compensate for workflow defects.

## Penalty controls

- Give each defect one primary home. Score another dimension only for a distinct consequence shown in the source.
- Compatibility is not explanation, and possibility does not establish probability.
- Familiar arguments receive no pedigree bonus.
- Fallacy and bias tags never add numerical penalties.
- Missing replies are recorded through response links, omission records, and coverage review—not fabricated counterpart moves.
- A complete structural answer may still be evidentially or logically poor.
- AI Extension material is outside the transcript and never improves or reduces participant scores.

## Aggregation

Move dimensions retain weights `25/20/20/15/10/10`. Sections are importance-weighted move means. Overall scores are prelocked section-weighted means plus only an eligible −5 to +5 debate-wide residual. No additional global coverage, coherence, resilience, style, or calibration meta-score is permitted.
