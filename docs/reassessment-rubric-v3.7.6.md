# Slugfester Burden-Contact Decomposition Rubric v3.7.6

Judge one complete anonymous composite candidate at a time.

## Exact contact

1. Identify the proposition actually asserted, denied, or undercut in the excerpt.
2. Route contact exists only when that proposition supplies a reason for or against one stated bridge claim, or directly attacks a necessary premise or inference of that bridge.
3. Shared topic, vocabulary, speaker side, responsiveness, relevance to the target, or a merely possible downstream implication is insufficient. When no bridge passes the proposition test, select no route contact.

## Polarity

1. Select `support` when the excerpt supplies a reason to accept the contacted bridge.
2. Select `attack` when it supplies a reason to reject, limit, or find insufficient the contacted bridge or a necessary inference within it.
3. The speaker's side is contextual evidence, not a substitute for expressed polarity.

## Bridge selection

1. Select the narrowest bridge whose stated proposition receives exact contact.
2. A direct attack belongs to the bridge attacked, even when the response suggests a different positive view.
3. Select a subsidiary bridge only when its specific proposition—not a generic “narrower consideration”—is expressed or attacked.
4. Select the motion bridge only when the move states, supports, or attacks the complete motion-level conclusion. Contact with one route premise does not inherit motion status.

Evidence must be one exact, case-sensitive substring occurring once in the source excerpt. The rationale must state the excerpt proposition, contact or no-contact rule, polarity when applicable, selected bridge claim, and exclusion of the nearest competitor.
