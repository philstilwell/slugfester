# Slugfester Route-Stratified Held-Out Five v4.2.21.8

This stage selects and screens the final end-to-end validation sample. It performs no model or audio call, derives no score, reads no legacy assessment content, and authorizes no execution.

The eligible population is limited to debates with exactly one speaker per side and a complete local transcript, event, and manifest chain. Every retired, development, prior fresh, rejected, and v4.2.20 consensus debate is excluded before ranking. Source files are mechanically hashed and measured, but transcript content is not semantically inspected during selection.

The frozen sample contains exactly five debates: two in the tested direct lane and three in the still-unvalidated partition lane. The deterministic tuple optimizer first maximizes distinct topic families, duration bins, caption kinds, distinct named speakers, and partition-severity bins. It then minimizes the aggregate salted rank and applies a debate-ID tie break. At least four topic families are required.

The route mix deliberately prevents the successful three-debate direct lane from masking the dominant unresolved corpus condition. Passing metadata screening authorizes partition-lane design and source-packet preparation only. It does not authorize primary execution, Pass B, audio transcription, adjudication, scoring, publication reconstruction, production mutation, or the 195-debate run.
