# Slugfester Disputed-Fields-Only Adjudication v4.2.21.17.5

The third 5.6 Sol pass receives one isolated context per debate. It sees only material disputed fields, independently anonymized candidate pairs, locked transcript evidence, and the two verified audio transcripts where required. Candidate provenance, initial pass identities and rationales, nondisputed fields, aggregate scores, winners, and publication prose remain unavailable.

Every required pair must be selected exactly once. The adjudicator may choose only an entire candidate object; it may not average, interpolate, rewrite, repair, or invent values. Importance is treated as a judgment field because it was not fixed by the score-blind inventory curator. Strict burden-adjustment exclusion remains in force.

The output remains score-blind. Final ledgers may be assembled only after every context validates, and scores may be derived only after every assembled ledger passes the unchanged source and semantic validator.
