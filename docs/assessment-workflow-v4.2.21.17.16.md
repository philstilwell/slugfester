# Slugfester Discovery Transport and Attribution Hardening v4.2.21.17.16

The replacement held-out gate produced two independent failure classes. All four contexts launched in the opening second timed out together, while thirteen of the fourteen later contexts validated. This supports a medium-confidence diagnosis of an opening transport stall, not a schema-wide incompatibility. Separately, the final chunk of Debate 193 promoted an audience questioner to a debate-side candidate; the deterministic speaker map correctly rejected it.

Future held-out and production launches require a structured-output canary drawn from retired material before any held-out context starts. After the canary validates, concurrency ramps from one context to two and then four. The held-out contexts still receive one attempt and no retry. Failed executions retain separate stdout and stderr tails, and the per-context timeout falls to five minutes so a transport stall cannot dominate the compute budget.

Generated candidate schemas now enumerate only the two frozen interlocutors. Audience and moderator material can remain in transcript context but cannot be emitted as candidates. The deterministic speaker-to-side validator remains as defense in depth.

The replacement five are retired from clean held-out status. Their thirteen valid contexts remain informative about schema behavior, but the gate does not authorize independent judgments, scores, publication, production mutation, or the 195-debate campaign.
