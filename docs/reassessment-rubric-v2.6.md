# Slugfester Reassessment Rubric v2.6

This classification-only rubric operationalizes the v2.6 target-contact repair. The v2.5 diagnostic, reframe, burden-route, evidence, and numerical-continuity rules remain unchanged.

## Step 1: target relation

Decide target relation before component contact.

Mark `substituted` only when the response changes one of these features:

- `object`: it answers a different claim or subject;
- `strength`: it replaces the target with a materially weaker or stronger proposition;
- `modality`: it changes possibility, probability, actuality, necessity, or certainty;
- `comparison-class`: it changes the relevant rival, baseline, population, or reference class; or
- `burden`: it replaces the live demand with a different party's burden.

A substituted judgment requires the shortest exact phrase showing the changed target. A poor, incomplete, indirect, or conditional attempt to answer the same claim remains `preserved`. Do not infer substitution merely from nonanswer.

When the target is substituted, do not annotate its components. When it is preserved, substitution type and evidence are null and every locked component must be evaluated.

## Step 2: typed component operation

For each component of a preserved target, choose one operation or null:

- `accepts`: explicitly grants or relies on that component as a premise;
- `denies`: explicitly asserts that the component is false;
- `distinguishes`: splits a term, case, scope, or referent in a way that directly changes that component;
- `qualifies`: narrows, weakens, conditions, or limits that component without replacing the target;
- `explains`: supplies a mechanism or reason that directly accounts for that component;
- `undermines`: attacks the warrant, inference, comparison, or evidential force of that component without directly denying it; or
- null: performs none of these operations on that component.

Every positive operation requires an exact phrase from the responding act. The phrase may be reused only when it explicitly ranges over multiple components and performs the selected operation on each.

## No-propagation and collective-reference rules

Target components form a directed graph. Contact is node-specific.

- An operation on an inference or conclusion does not contact its premises.
- An operation on a premise does not contact a dependent inference or conclusion unless the act separately performs an operation on that node.
- Referring to a conjunction, theory, story, or case does not accept every constituent fact merely because those facts appear in the target packet.
- A collective phrase contacts multiple nodes only when its grammatical and argumentative scope clearly includes each node and the same operation applies to each.
- Repeating, mentioning, or presupposing a component for orientation is not `accepts` unless the act grants or relies on it.

When two operations seem possible, choose the one most directly expressed by the cited phrase. Operation choice does not change derived contact; a non-null qualifying operation always derives `addressed`.

## Relevant contrary material

Use relevant contrary material only when the target is preserved and every component operation is null. It requires an exact phrase that bears on the preserved target without accepting, denying, distinguishing, qualifying, explaining, or undermining a component. Topic overlap or a promise to answer later is false.

## Typed target graph

Inventory builders use the narrowest component kind supported by the opponent's act:

- `fact-premise`: an asserted observation, event, datum, or descriptive premise;
- `rule-comparison`: a norm, baseline, reference class, conditional, or comparative standard;
- `inference`: a stated step from other locked nodes;
- `burden`: a demand about what must be shown or excluded;
- `modality`: a distinct possibility, probability, necessity, or certainty claim; and
- `conclusion`: the target's terminal claim.

Inference and conclusion nodes must name their dependencies. Do not duplicate a conclusion as both a fact and an inference, split one proposition into stylistic paraphrases, or treat an entailed implication as independently indispensable without a stated dependency.

The immediate prior opponent claim is the default target. An earlier claim is permitted only when the response explicitly returns to it and the inventory records the intervening-claim exception.

## Unchanged mechanism and burden anchors

Apply the v2.5 defect-type, explicit-impact, malformed-demand, replacement-demand, evidence-offset, eligible-bridge, and bridge-tier rules without alteration. Diagnostic, reframe, coverage, and burden relation remain calculator-derived.

## Numerical continuity if later authorized

Derived coverage would constrain responsiveness exactly as before: constructive `0–100`, full `80–100`, partial `55–79`, relevant-nonanswer `50–69`, substitution `0–49`.

Derived burden relation would constrain relevance/burden exactly as before: completes `90–100`, advances-central `80–89`, advances-sub-burden `70–79`, topical-peripheral `50–69`, unadopted-or-irrelevant `0–49`.

No v2.6 artifact authorizes numerical scoring by itself.
