# Slugfester Reassessment Rubric v3.0

Rubric v3.0 retains the v2.9.1 scoring-relevant primitives and makes the final adjudicated consensus lock—not either raw pass—the sole structural input to later participant-performance scoring.

## Primitive decision order

1. Mark original-target contact only when exact response language bears on the locked claim or one of its indispensable components.
2. Mark a connected example only when the response expressly links another case, analogy, counterexample, or model to the target.
3. When target contact is true, classify scope in this order: modality change, narrower nonmodal range, stronger nonmodal commitment, or same.
4. Retain the burden unless the same demand is expressly transferred or a materially different governing success condition is expressly installed.
5. Mark contact independently for every indispensable component. Do not propagate contact through dependencies.
6. Select an expressly cued defect and then independently locate a separate clause stating what consequently fails, does not follow, is not established, does not explain, cannot carry the claimed weight, or must be limited.
7. Require both an explanation of malformed framing and a stated replacement demand for a reframe.
8. Select only the highest evidenced eligible burden tier.

## Coverage and responsiveness anchors

Code derives coverage after the consensus merge:

- `full`: every indispensable component is contacted;
- `partial`: at least one but not every component is contacted;
- `relevant-nonanswer`: the target or relevant contrary material is contacted without component contact; and
- `nonanswer`: neither the target nor relevant contrary material is contacted.

For later scoring, the final lock constrains responsiveness:

| Final structural result | Permitted responsiveness band |
| --- | ---: |
| Full component coverage, or a complete diagnostic/reframe that defeats the decisive live demand | 80–100 |
| Partial component coverage | 55–79 |
| Relevant nonanswer or counterargument that bears on the issue without answering the locked components | 40–69 |
| Nonanswer | 0–39 |

A complete diagnostic or reframe does not automatically receive a high number; its reasoning must still be accurate and consequential. Constructive moves are evaluated against the motion and their locked burden rather than an earlier opponent target.

## Burden relevance anchors

The highest final burden tier constrains relevance/burden:

| Final burden relation | Permitted relevance/burden band |
| --- | ---: |
| Completes the motion-level route | 90–100 |
| Advances a central bridge | 75–89 |
| Advances a subsidiary bridge | 55–74 |
| Topical but peripheral to an adopted route | 25–54 |
| Unadopted, invented, or irrelevant burden | 0–24 |

Supporting and attacking an eligible bridge are equally capable of burden contact. Direction and quality are assessed elsewhere; they do not change the tier.

## Burden-adjustment exclusion

The default full-debate adjustment is zero. A nonzero adjustment from −5 to +5 is eligible only when all of the following are true:

- the consequence is debate-wide and distinct from any selected move judgment;
- it changes whether a locked burden's explicit success criterion is completed;
- it is not already captured by responsiveness, relevance/burden, another dimension, section weighting, move importance, an omission link, or another adjustment; and
- the ledger identifies the affected burden, related moves, counterfactual effect, and exact reason the consequence survives the duplicate-penalty exclusion.

If either raw pass fails any eligibility condition, or the passes identify materially different consequences, the final adjustment is zero unless that exact eligibility dispute is exposed and resolved in the dispute-only adjudication. Style, eloquence, worldview plausibility, general coherence, and a second penalty for an already scored omission are categorically excluded.

## Partial-answer anchor

A reply is partial only when exact language contacts at least one indispensable target component and leaves another indispensable component untouched. Answering a weaker reconstruction, offering nearby contrary material, or changing the success condition is not partial coverage unless a locked component is actually contacted. A compound target with one ornamental clause should not be used; components must be independently necessary to the target's success.

## Charity and calibration anchor

Later performance scoring separates:

- epistemic calibration: whether confidence, modality, and stated uncertainty fit the offered support; and
- representational charity: whether the move addresses the strongest live version actually advanced, including relevant concessions and qualifications.

Politeness, warmth, abrasiveness, and rhetorical confidence are not charity. A critical paraphrase can be charitable when it preserves the opponent's operative commitments; a courteous straw man is not. The combined dimension remains the rounded mean of the two subratings.

## Evidence and defaults

Positive or changed primitives require the shortest complete exact clause that preserves controlling negation, modality, quantifier, comparison, condition, and referent. Offsets are zero-based and end-exclusive into the supplied response excerpt.

When an explicitness test fails, use the default: target contact false, connected example false, scope same, burden retained, all components false, no relevant contrary material, no defect, no consequence, no malformed-demand explanation, no replacement demand, and burden tier none. Defaults are disciplined abstention, not negative participant-performance scores.

## Consensus rule

The two raw passes have equal standing. Agreement is copied mechanically. Disagreement is not averaged, majority-voted, or resolved by confidence labels; it is sent to the isolated dispute-only adjudicator. Only the validated final consensus lock may constrain or supply later scores.
