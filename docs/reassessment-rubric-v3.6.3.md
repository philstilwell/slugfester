# Slugfester Reassessment Rubric v3.6.3

Version 3.6.3 retains the v3.6 decision anchors and adds two mechanical safeguards.

Evidence uniqueness means exactly one raw, case-sensitive substring occurrence in `sourceExcerpt`, including occurrences embedded inside longer words. Expand a short cue with adjacent source words until it occurs once. Conditional evidence fields follow the schema exactly: inactive and exclusion states are null.

Return only the requested decision card. Do not emit a score, critique, winner, Overall Commentary, AI Extension, legacy prose, or derived annotation.
