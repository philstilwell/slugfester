# Slugfester Reassessment Rubric v3.6

Version 3.6 governs targeted classification cards only. It inherits v3.5 compiler invariants and v3.4 substantive anchors, but it does not select or expose participant-performance scores.

## Target, components, and examples

Direct target contact requires exact language taking up the locked proposition. Each component starts at `none` and receives a positive mode only when its exact proposition is asserted, denied, restricted, distinguished, explained, or challenged. Explicit global assent requires language ranging over the complete locked proposition; generic assent does not distribute.

An example is `distinct-connected-example` only when it introduces another case, analogy, counterexample, or model bearing on the target. Material already inside the locked target is `inside-locked-target`. Component contact mechanically precludes contrary-material credit.

## Diagnostics

Find the cue before choosing a label. A denial, alternative view, change of subject, or mere request for support is not a diagnosis.

A consequence is positive only when a separate clause states what fails because of the defect. The card must identify the exact linguistic link joining defect and consequence; proximity alone is insufficient. Defect and consequence remain one atomic source bundle.

## Reframes

Malformed-demand credit requires an explanation of why the governing question or success condition is defective. Replacement credit requires an explicit alternative governing question or condition. When both are claimed, identify the exact language linking the criticism to the replacement.

## Burden conflicts

Burden adjustment remains retained absent explicit language transferring the same demand or installing a different governing condition. Burden contact requires exact support for or attack on an eligible listed bridge. Topical relevance and rhetorical force are insufficient.

## Charity and exclusion

Choose the strongest category licensed by the words without inventing a component, cue, link, consequence, replacement, or bridge. No numerical score, score band selection, critique, Overall Commentary, AI Extension, or winner may appear in a v3.6 decision-card artifact.
