# Slugfester Taxonomy-and-Priority Correction Workflow v3.7.5

Version 3.7.5 is an exposed development correction smoke for the two rule ambiguities observed in v3.7.4. It tests four diagnostic classifications and four burden-route selections. The cases are intentionally reused development material, so a pass cannot establish generalization, correct a benchmark key, or authorize scoring or production work.

Two isolated 5.6 Sol contexts are planned per debate. Anonymous candidate positions are counterbalanced and origins are sealed. A third isolated context may receive only disputed bundles. Every final bundle requires two matching votes, while the correction itself requires perfect eight-of-eight initial agreement.

There is one attempt per context, no retry or repair, and no score or assessment prose may be derived. Medium-confidence attribution would require audio verification; all sampled moves are high confidence.
