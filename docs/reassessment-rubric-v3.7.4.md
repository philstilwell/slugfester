# Slugfester Disjoint Retired Atomic-Bundle Rubric v3.7.4

Judge one complete anonymous candidate at a time.

- **Target contact:** require explicit propositional contact for each component. Topic overlap, a related example, or contact with another component does not propagate. `distinction` requires an express contrast with the component; otherwise default to `none`.
- **Diagnostic classification:** identify the exact defect cue. `ambiguity` requires an equivocation or conflation; `attribution-error` requires assigning a claim or category to the wrong source; `invalid-inference` requires an express inferential complaint. Default to `none` when no cue is present.
- **Linked consequence:** choose a non-`none` relation only when the excerpt states both the defect and what follows from it under an explicit link. Do not turn temporal proximity into linkage.
- **Reframe:** a non-`none` relation requires both an identified malformed demand and an expressly stated replacement demand. Partial cues retain relation `none`.
- **Burden relevance:** choose the strongest route bridge the move actually advances. Responsiveness, target contact, and topic overlap alone do not establish burden contact; prefer the narrower supported tier when a broader tier is not itself advanced.

Select the strongest licensed complete tuple, not the option with the most positive fields or the broadest burden tier. Evidence must be one exact, case-sensitive substring occurring once in the source excerpt. Each rationale must state the positive rule, the default, and the exclusion that defeats the nearest alternative.
