# Slugfester Reassessment Rubric v2.1

Score the argumentative performance present in the locked source—not whether a worldview is true, admirable, popular, or familiar. A score is earned move by move and has no automatic floor.

## Shared band anchors

| Band | Operational meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 95–100 | Exceptional and unusually complete: explicit inferential structure, strong public warrant, direct engagement with the strongest live reply, and little material repair needed. |
| 85–94 | Very strong: the move substantially advances its burden and survives the best live objection, with only bounded residual gaps. |
| 75–84 | Strong/competent: coherent and relevant, but compressed, incompletely warranted, or vulnerable at one important bridge. |
| 65–74 | Mixed: a useful core is present, yet a material premise, inference, or reply remains underdeveloped. |
| 50–64 | Weak: a major burden is not discharged, or the move largely misses the live issue despite some relevant content. |
| 25–49 | Very weak: the inference is seriously defective, substantially unsupported, or mostly unresponsive. |
| 0–24 | Non-performance: no usable argument on the assigned issue, a self-defeating contradiction, or content that is essentially irrelevant to the burden. |

Use 50, 70, 80, and 90 as decision boundaries, not default magnets. Before moving a dimension by five points, identify the particular transcript evidence or missing bridge that warrants the change.

## Dimensions

### Logical coherence — 25%

- **90+**: premises, intermediate steps, scope, and conclusion are explicit and mutually consistent; the strongest live validity challenge is answered.
- **75–89**: the inference is intelligible and mostly valid but leaves one bridge implicit or one ambiguity unresolved.
- **50–74**: the conclusion depends on a material equivocation, unsupported conditional, false dilemma, internal tension, or non sequitur.
- **Below 50**: the move is self-undermining, plainly invalid at its core, or lacks an inferential route to its conclusion.

Do not lower this dimension merely because a premise lacks evidence; that belongs primarily under evidence/warrant.

### Evidence and warrant — 20%

- **90+**: relevant evidence is specific, sourceable, representative, and connected to the conclusion through defended warrants and serious alternative comparison.
- **75–89**: appropriate support is offered, but documentation, independence, base rates, rival hypotheses, or a key warrant remains compressed.
- **50–74**: authority, anecdote, assertion, selective examples, or speculative mechanisms carry substantial weight without adequate checking.
- **Below 50**: a fact-dependent conclusion is effectively unsupported or relies on evidence contradicted within the locked record.

Lack of an inline citation is not itself disproof. Score the degree to which verification matters to the inference.

### Responsiveness — 20%

- **90+**: accurately identifies and answers the opponent's strongest relevant claim at its decisive premise or inference.
- **75–89**: directly engages the live issue but leaves a major component unanswered or answers it only conditionally.
- **50–74**: responds to a secondary point, a weaker reconstruction, or only one part of a compound objection.
- **Below 50**: evades, changes the subject, or attacks a position the opponent did not advance.

Constructive openings are scored for responsiveness to the motion and known burden, not penalized for lacking an earlier opponent move.

### Relevance and burden — 15%

- **90+**: directly discharges a locked burden or decisively defeats an opponent burden, with a conclusion proportionate to what that success establishes.
- **75–89**: materially advances the burden but does not complete it or leaves one required link unstated.
- **50–74**: related to the topic but peripheral, burden-shifting, or stronger in rhetoric than in motion-level consequence.
- **Below 50**: irrelevant to the locked motion or depends on assigning the opponent a burden they did not adopt.

Judge opportunity fairly. Do not invent a positive contrary burden for a side whose declared role is critical.

### Precision and clarity — 10%

- **90+**: key terms, modality, comparison class, scope, and confidence are stable and easy to audit.
- **75–89**: the move is understandable with only minor ambiguity or compression.
- **50–74**: unstable terminology, vague quantifiers, unclear referents, or missing distinctions materially obscure evaluation.
- **Below 50**: the claim cannot be reconstructed reliably enough to assess.

Fluency, wit, and polish are not clarity unless they improve the recoverable argument.

### Calibration and charity — 10%

- **90+**: confidence matches the evidence, concessions are accurate, alternatives are represented in their strongest live form, and uncertainty is explicit.
- **75–89**: generally proportionate and fair, with limited overstatement or incomplete treatment of an alternative.
- **50–74**: significant overclaiming, selective skepticism, motive attribution, or an avoidably weak representation of the alternative.
- **Below 50**: certainty is radically disproportionate or the move depends on a gross caricature.

Do not award charity merely for politeness or deduct it for an abrasive tone unless the tone changes the argumentative representation.

## Penalty controls

- Identify one primary home for each defect. Apply effects in additional dimensions only when the transcript shows genuinely distinct consequences.
- Compatibility is not explanation; possibility can answer contradiction without establishing probability.
- Familiar named arguments receive no pedigree bonus. Score only the version actually presented.
- A fallacy or bias tag never creates an extra deduction.
- Audience response, status, accent, speed, and confidence are excluded.
- Missing replies are represented by response links and burden completion; do not manufacture paired moves.
- Score diagnostic questions as questions or challenges, not as unargued contrary theories.

## Aggregation

Move scores use fixed dimension weights. Section scores are rounded importance-weighted means of selected moves. Overall scores are prelocked section-weighted means plus a −5 to +5 burden-completion adjustment. No additional coverage, coherence, resilience, or global-calibration meta-score is added; those qualities are already visible in the moves and burden record.
