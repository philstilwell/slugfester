# Slugfester Reassessment Rubric v2.8

This classification-only rubric applies the v2.8 decision sequence. It evaluates the response actually made against the locked target and burden; it does not evaluate whether either worldview is true.

## 1. Proposition mapping

Write a one-clause internal paraphrase of the target proposition and the response proposition. Choose `same` unless substituting the response proposition into the target changes the subject, referent, comparison class, baseline, or question type.

Use:

- `subject`: a different entity or phenomenon now bears the predicate;
- `referent`: the same word or pronoun points to a different thing;
- `comparison-class`: different alternatives or classes are compared;
- `baseline`: the comparison is evaluated against a different reference point; or
- `question-type`: the response answers a different kind of question.

A mapped counterexample, analogy, instance, or rival model remains the same object when the speaker expressly connects it to the target inference. Mere topical similarity does not.

## 2. Scope

For the mapped proposition only:

- `same`: no material change in range, strength, condition, time, population, or modality;
- `narrowed`: restricts one of those features;
- `strengthened`: adopts a materially stronger commitment; or
- `modality-shift`: changes possibility, probability, actuality, necessity, or certainty.

Scope cannot turn a changed object into the same object. A scope change on a preserved target must be reflected by a qualifying or distinguishing component operation.

## 3. Burden

- `retained`: continues to answer the locked live demand;
- `reassigned`: explicitly transfers that same demand to another party;
- `replaced`: explicitly supplies a different success condition or demand.

Counterquestions, symmetry charges, and competing claims retain the burden unless an exact clause performs the transfer or replacement.

## 4. Components and operation precedence

Classify each indispensable component independently. Use one primary operation:

1. `denies` for an express falsity claim;
2. `accepts` for an express grant or reliance;
3. `qualifies` for a limitation of scope, strength, modality, condition, time, or population;
4. `distinguishes` for a split among meanings, cases, referents, or comparison classes;
5. `undermines` for an attack on warrant, inference, baseline, or evidential force;
6. `explains` for a mechanism or reason that treats the component as a datum; or
7. null for no express operation.

Do not infer contact from a graph dependency. Do not credit the same words to multiple components unless their grammatical and argumentative scope covers each one.

An example or analogy contacts a component only when the speaker states or unmistakably completes its mapping to that component. Otherwise it may be relevant contrary material.

## 5. Evidence selection

Evidence is zero-based and end-exclusive into the responding excerpt.

Choose the shortest complete clause that preserves controlling:

- negation;
- modality;
- quantifier;
- comparison;
- condition; and
- named referent.

Prefer an explicit noun phrase to a pronoun-only fragment. If two spans are equally complete, choose the shorter; if still tied, choose the earlier. Evidence-boundary disagreement is audited separately from semantic classification.

## 6. Coverage

Coverage is derived:

- `substitution`: object changed or burden reassigned/replaced;
- `full`: every component contacted;
- `partial`: at least one but not every component contacted;
- `relevant-nonanswer`: no component contacted, but exact contrary material bears on the target;
- `nonanswer`: preserved target without contact or qualifying contrary material; and
- `not-applicable`: constructive move.

## 7. Diagnostic cue

Do not infer a diagnostic merely because the response disagrees or supplies a counterargument. A non-none defect requires exact words expressing the corresponding cue.

| Defect | Cue test |
| --- | --- |
| `contradiction` | Does the move say the target's commitments cannot jointly hold? |
| `missing-premise` | Does it say a necessary supporting proposition is absent? |
| `ambiguity` | Does it identify an unstable term, scope, reading, or referent? |
| `invalid-inference` | Does it say the conclusion does not follow even granting the premises? |
| `unsupported-comparison` | Does it identify a missing or nontransferable baseline or rival comparison? |
| `irrelevance` | Does it say the offered material does not bear on the conclusion or burden? |
| `evidential-insufficiency` | Does it say the evidence does not warrant the claim? |
| `scope-mismatch` | Does it say the target demands or concludes more than the relevant scope permits? |
| `attribution-error` | Does the respondent expressly disown the attributed claim? |

Select the packet only for whole-argument criticism. Select a component when the expressed criticism attaches to that component. A missing premise is not itself an existing component; use the inference, conclusion, burden, or packet that requires it.

## 8. Impact

- `none`: no evaluative or inferential result;
- `verdict`: a bare negative judgment; or
- `inferential-consequence`: an exact clause states why the target object, inference, conclusion, comparison, explanation, attribution, or burden fails or is limited.

“Wrong,” “absurd,” “inadequate,” “not good enough,” ridicule, and contradiction-by-example are verdicts or ordinary counterargument unless the speaker states the inferential result.

The derived diagnostic is true only when an eligible non-none defect and object have an explicit inferential consequence.

## 9. Reframe

A reframe requires both:

1. exact language explaining why the original demand is malformed; and
2. exact language stating the replacement demand.

Topical redirection, a counterquestion, or a new argument without both clauses is not a reframe.

## 10. Burden relation

Credit only locked eligible bridges:

- motion bridge → `completes`;
- central bridge → `advances-central`;
- subsidiary bridge → `advances-sub-burden`;
- no bridge but topical material → `topical-peripheral`; or
- no adopted route → `unadopted-or-irrelevant`.

Side membership never transfers individual move ownership.

## 11. Multi-speaker routing

Use the locked opponent map. A response to a teammate is excluded from opponent-responsive credit. A teammate's claim remains speaker-only unless an exact adoption record precedes the response. Moderator prompts do not become participant claims.

## Numerical stop

This rubric contains no numerical performance anchors and authorizes no scores. It feeds only the v2.8 classification and reliability gates.
