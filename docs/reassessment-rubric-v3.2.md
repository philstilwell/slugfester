# Slugfester Reassessment Rubric v3.2

Rubric v3.2 retains the v3.1 scoring-relevant primitives and replaces universal verifier authority with conservative, risk-triggered adjudication.

## Default-first decision order

1. Read the locked target, indispensable components, response, and eligible burden route literally.
2. Start from the field default: false, `same`, `retained`, `none`, or burden tier `none`.
3. Identify exact response language satisfying the positive or changed-value rule.
4. Apply the field's near-miss exclusions before leaving the default.
5. Preserve controlling negation, modality, quantifier, comparison, condition, and referent in the shortest complete evidence clause.

Defaults are disciplined abstention, not low participant scores.

## Targeting and burden

- **Target contact:** true only when the response bears on the locked proposition or an indispensable component. Topic similarity is insufficient.
- **Connected example:** true only when a distinct case, analogy, counterexample, or model is expressly used to bear on the target. Merely naming people already inside the target, mentioning another topic, or giving an illustration with no explicit connection is insufficient.
- **Scope:** use `modality-shift` only for an expressed change among possibility, probability, necessity, or certainty. Use `narrowed` or `strengthened` only when the response changes the range or commitment of the same contacted proposition. A new topic, a more interesting question, or a different evaluative concern is not a scope relation.
- **Burden adjustment:** default `retained`. `reassigned` requires express transfer of the same demand. `replaced` requires an expressly installed, materially different governing success condition. A counterargument, request for evidence, subsidiary issue, or narrower conclusion is insufficient.
- **Burden contact:** choose the highest eligible bridge expressly supported or attacked. Critical contact counts symmetrically with constructive support; do not require the speaker to repeat the bridge wording.

## Coverage and partial answers

Judge every indispensable component independently. Contact requires proposition-level engagement: granting, using, denying, restricting, distinguishing, explaining, questioning the warrant of, or expressly challenging that component. Shared vocabulary is only a routing cue and never proves contact. Do not propagate contact through dependencies.

- `full`: every indispensable component is contacted;
- `partial`: at least one but not every component is contacted;
- `relevant-nonanswer`: no component is contacted, but the target or relevant contrary material is expressly addressed; and
- `nonanswer`: neither the target nor relevant contrary material is contacted.

## Eligible diagnostic defects

Use only the first defect clearly expressed in the response:

- `attribution-error`: the opponent's position, commitment, or claim is represented incorrectly;
- `contradiction`: two operative claims cannot both be true in the asserted respect;
- `ambiguity`: a material expression or distinction has unresolved readings;
- `scope-mismatch`: the conclusion's range or modality exceeds or differs from what the premise supports;
- `unsupported-comparison`: a comparison lacks the required basis, class, or control;
- `missing-premise`: an indispensable inferential bridge is unstated;
- `invalid-inference`: the stated conclusion does not follow from the stated reasons;
- `evidential-insufficiency`: the offered reasons are too weak to establish the conclusion;
- `irrelevance`: the offered reason does not bear on the operative issue; or
- `none`: no eligible defect is expressly identified.

A denial, contrary assertion, example, request for more detail, or alternative question does not by itself identify a defect. A complete diagnostic additionally requires a separate clause stating what consequently fails, does not follow, is not established, does not explain, cannot carry the claimed weight, or must be limited. The consequence must be linked to the defect rather than merely repeat the speaker's contrary conclusion.

## Reframe

Judge the two primitives separately:

1. the response explains why the governing demand or framing is malformed; and
2. it states a replacement governing demand.

A different question, alternative topic, narrower claim, or statement that another issue is more interesting is not a reframe without an explanation of the original framing defect. Criticism without replacement is incomplete; replacement without the malformed-demand explanation is redirection.

## Charity, calibration, and burden adjustment

Charity concerns accurate representation of the strongest live version, not politeness. Calibration concerns whether confidence and modality fit the support. The full-debate burden-completion adjustment defaults to zero and remains eligible only when it changes an explicit debate-wide success criterion, is distinct from every selected move judgment, and is absent from all other dimensions, weights, omissions, and adjustments. Failure of any condition forces zero.

## Adjudication rule

For a semantic conflict, the adjudicator selects A or B only. For a risk-disputed agreement, retaining the shared value is the default. Override requires satisfaction of the exact field rule and its exclusions; philosophical sophistication, general relevance, or a plausible reconstruction cannot substitute for explicit source support. Unflagged agreement is copied mechanically and cannot be altered.
