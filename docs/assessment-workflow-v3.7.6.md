# Slugfester Burden-Contact Decomposition Workflow v3.7.6

Version 3.7.6 is an exposed, design-only correction for the remaining burden-route instability observed in v3.7.5. It preserves the stable v3.7.5 diagnostic precedence and changes only burden contact.

Each case is decided as one valid composite: no route contact, support for one exact bridge, or attack on one exact bridge. This prevents the scoring pass from separately producing incompatible contact, polarity, and bridge values. Candidate positions are counterbalanced, origins and provisional design fixtures are sealed, and no generic subsidiary catch-all is permitted.

Two isolated 5.6 Sol contexts per debate are contemplated only after a separate execution lock. A third isolated context may receive only disputed composite cases. Perfect initial agreement on all eight cases is required; final decisions require two matching votes. This exposed correction cannot establish human ground truth, generalization, scoring validity, or production readiness.

There is one attempt per context, no retry or repair, and no scores or assessment prose may be derived. Medium-confidence attribution requires audio verification; all development cases are high confidence.
