# Slugfester Taxonomy-and-Priority Correction Rubric v3.7.5

Judge one complete anonymous candidate at a time.

## Diagnostic precedence

1. Choose `attribution-error` when the speaker says a proposition, category, or view is being assigned or placed onto the wrong person, object, source, or framework. This takes precedence over the fact that the mistaken assignment may also conflate concepts.
2. Choose `ambiguity` when the complaint is primarily that a term changes meaning or that two concepts are confused, without an express wrong-source or wrong-object assignment.
3. Choose `invalid-inference` when the complaint primarily identifies an unsupported or self-defeating premise-to-conclusion step.
4. Default to `none` when the excerpt contains no explicit defect cue.

Anchors: “put morality onto dharma” is attribution-error; “confusing causation and control” is ambiguity; “the fall does not explain … because Eve already had it” is invalid-inference.

## Burden-route priority

1. Identify the move's primary argumentative function. A direct attack on a route premise or inference is assigned to that attacked bridge before any different bridge the response may incidentally support.
2. Assign a subsidiary alternative only when the move independently advances that alternative, not merely when it uses the alternative to rebut a central bridge.
3. Assign the motion tier only when the move itself states or directly establishes the complete motion-level result; do not promote a central attack merely because it could reach the motion.
4. When no central or motion bridge is advanced, prefer the narrower supported subsidiary tier. Topic overlap and responsiveness alone are excluded.

Evidence must be one exact, case-sensitive substring occurring once in the source excerpt. Each rationale must state the positive rule, the default, and why the nearest competing classification or bridge is excluded.
