# Slugfester Reassessment Rubric v2.2

Score the argumentative performance in the locked source—not whether a worldview is true, admirable, popular, or familiar. A score is earned move by move and has no automatic floor. This version sharpens the dimensions that produced disagreement in the v2.1 complete-debate gate while leaving the six dimension weights unchanged.

## Shared band anchors

| Band | Operational meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 95–100 | Exceptional and unusually complete: explicit inferential structure, strong public warrant, direct engagement with the strongest live reply, and little material repair needed. |
| 85–94 | Very strong: the move substantially advances its burden and survives the best live objection, with only bounded residual gaps. |
| 75–84 | Strong/competent: coherent and relevant, but compressed, incompletely warranted, or vulnerable at one important bridge. |
| 65–74 | Mixed: a useful core is present, yet a material premise, inference, or reply remains underdeveloped. |
| 50–64 | Weak: a major burden is not discharged, or the move largely misses the live issue despite some relevant content. |
| 25–49 | Very weak: the inference is seriously defective, substantially unsupported, or mostly unresponsive. |
| 0–24 | Non-performance: no usable argument on the assigned issue, a self-defeating contradiction, or content that is essentially irrelevant to the burden. |

Use 50, 70, 80, and 90 as decision boundaries, not default magnets. Before moving a dimension by five points, identify the transcript evidence or missing bridge that warrants the change.

## Dimensions

### Logical coherence — 25%

- **90+**: premises, intermediate steps, scope, and conclusion are explicit and mutually consistent; the strongest live validity challenge is answered.
- **75–89**: the inference is intelligible and mostly valid but leaves one bridge implicit or one ambiguity unresolved.
- **50–74**: the conclusion depends on a material equivocation, unsupported conditional, false dilemma, internal tension, or non sequitur.
- **Below 50**: the move is self-undermining, plainly invalid at its core, or lacks an inferential route to its conclusion.

Do not lower this dimension merely because a premise lacks evidence; that belongs primarily under evidence/warrant.

### Evidence and warrant — 20%

- **90+**: relevant evidence is specific, sourceable, representative, and connected to the conclusion through defended warrants and serious alternative comparison.
- **75–89**: appropriate support is offered, but documentation, independence, base rates, rival hypotheses, or a key warrant remains compressed.
- **50–74**: authority, anecdote, assertion, selective examples, or speculative mechanisms carry substantial weight without adequate checking.
- **Below 50**: a fact-dependent conclusion is effectively unsupported or relies on evidence contradicted within the locked record.

Lack of an inline citation is not itself disproof. Score the degree to which verification matters to the inference.

### Responsiveness — 20%

- **90+**: answers the strongest live claim at its decisive premise or inference, including every component needed for that answer to succeed.
- **80–89**: directly answers the core claim but leaves one material subpoint or consequence open.
- **70–79**: gives a direct but partial answer, or a justified reframe that resolves only part of the corrected issue.
- **50–69**: offers a relevant counterpoint without answering the central question, or answers a secondary or materially weaker reconstruction.
- **Below 50**: evades, changes the subject, substitutes an unrelated burden, or attacks a position the opponent did not advance.

Classify the response before choosing a number:

| Response class | Operational test | Responsiveness ceiling |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Full direct answer | Resolves the strongest live claim at the point on which it succeeds or fails. | None |
| Partial direct answer | Answers the central claim but leaves a necessary component unresolved. | 79 |
| Relevant counterargument | Supplies relevant contrary material without answering the live question or objection. | 69 |
| Diagnostic question | Exposes a missing premise, ambiguity, or consequence but does not itself supply a complete answer. | 74, unless the diagnosis itself defeats the burden |
| Topic shift or weaker substitution | Changes the issue or attacks a position materially weaker than the one stated. | 49 |

A reframe escapes the applicable ceiling only when the speaker explains why the original demand is malformed and then answers the corrected issue. Constructive openings are scored for responsiveness to the motion and adopted burden, not penalized for lacking an earlier opponent move.

Apply a partial-answer defect primarily here. Do not repeat it under coherence or relevance unless the locked span shows a distinct invalid inference or burden consequence.

### Relevance and burden — 15%

- **90+**: completes an adopted locked burden or decisively defeats an opponent burden, with a motion-level consequence proportionate to that success.
- **80–89**: materially advances the central burden, with only one bounded bridge or consequence unstated.
- **70–79**: advances a necessary sub-burden but does not establish the motion-level consequence.
- **50–69**: is topical but peripheral, shifts the burden, or addresses a burden the side did not adopt.
- **Below 50**: is irrelevant to the locked motion or depends on assigning the opponent a positive burden they did not accept.

Judge the move against the burden IDs and success criteria locked before scoring. A critic does not acquire the burden of proving the contrary merely by challenging a constructive case. In a multi-speaker side, judge each move against that speaker's stated position and the genuinely shared side burden. Do not penalize one speaker because another allied speaker adopts a different theory unless the move relies on a joint claim.

Score only the selected move span. Do not import a later consequence, an earlier omission, or another participant's position into the move's burden score.

### Precision and clarity — 10%

- **90+**: key terms, modality, comparison class, scope, and confidence are stable and easy to audit.
- **75–89**: the move is understandable with only minor ambiguity or compression.
- **50–74**: unstable terminology, vague quantifiers, unclear referents, or missing distinctions materially obscure evaluation.
- **Below 50**: the claim cannot be reconstructed reliably enough to assess.

Fluency, wit, and polish are not clarity unless they improve the recoverable argument.

### Calibration and charity — 10%

- **90+**: uncertainty is explicit and proportionate, concessions are accurate, and the strongest live alternative is represented before criticism.
- **80–89**: the move is fair and proportionate, with only a minor overstatement or omitted caveat.
- **70–79**: the representation is mostly fair, but the conclusion goes one material step beyond the warrant or a necessary caveat is absent.
- **50–69**: the move depends on a weaker reconstruction, selective skepticism, substantial overclaiming, or unsupported motive attribution.
- **Below 50**: certainty is radically disproportionate or the move depends on a gross caricature of the live position.

Politeness is not charity, and abrasiveness is not a deduction unless it changes the argumentative representation. Do not infer intent. Advocacy confidence is not overconfidence when the locked span supplies commensurate warrant. Evaluate only what the selected span says; do not repair or condemn it with material from elsewhere in the debate.

## Penalty controls

- Identify one primary home for each defect. Apply effects in additional dimensions only when the transcript shows genuinely distinct consequences.
- Compatibility is not explanation; possibility can answer contradiction without establishing probability.
- Familiar named arguments receive no pedigree bonus. Score only the version actually presented.
- A fallacy or bias tag never creates an extra deduction.
- Audience response, status, accent, speed, confidence, and style are excluded.
- Missing replies are represented by response links and burden completion; do not manufacture paired moves.
- Score diagnostic questions as questions or challenges, not as unargued contrary theories.
- Every rationale must name the response class when responsiveness is below 90 and must identify the locked burden consequence when relevance/burden is 80 or higher.

## Aggregation

Move scores use fixed dimension weights. Section scores are rounded importance-weighted means of selected moves. Overall scores are prelocked section-weighted means plus a −5 to +5 burden-completion adjustment. No additional coverage, coherence, resilience, or global-calibration meta-score is added; those qualities are already visible in the moves and burden record.
