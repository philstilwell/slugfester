# Slugfester Lean Integrated Primary Benchmark v4.2.12

This retired benchmark repeats Debate 99's successful integrated-primary judgment with a smaller output contract. The v4.2.10 judge spent 5.94 minutes producing a complete 64 KB primary object, including source-discovery facts already fixed by the validated candidate proposals.

The v4.2.12 judge still receives all 36 proposals, exact source excerpts, a six-event context margin around every proposal, the complete scoring rubrics, and the debate packet. It selects and evaluates the minimum complete move inventory. It emits routes, sections, rewritten propositions and attribution rationales, response analysis, burden contacts, precision and calibration findings, charity checks, ratings, evidence rationales, confidence, adjustment exclusions, and the audit attestation.

Repository code—not the judge—restores each selected candidate's side, speaker, move kind, exact source span, exact source excerpt, and attribution-confidence level. It then runs the unchanged v4.2.6 complete-source validator and compiler. This removes repetition without weakening candidate provenance, transcript grounding, scoring anchors, or the requirement to flag medium-confidence attribution for later audio verification.

One Sol/low attempt is allowed. The prior v4.2.10 result is a validation-only fixture and is unavailable to the judge. A valid result must shorten Debate 99's successful lane enough for the existing central and conservative projections, including the measured v4.2.11 correction overhead, to remain at or below 52 and 60 compute-hours respectively. The benchmark does not authorize scores, Pass B, production mutation, or the 195-debate run.
