# Slugfester Reassessment Rubric v2.3

Score only the argumentative performance in the locked source. Do not score a worldview's truth, popularity, moral attractiveness, pedigree, or the speaker's style. Response relation is already locked by a separate two-pass classification stage and may not be relabeled during numerical scoring.

## Shared anchors

| Band | Operational meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 95–100 | Exceptional and unusually complete; little material repair is needed. |
| 85–94 | Very strong; the move substantially advances its burden with only bounded residual gaps. |
| 75–84 | Strong/competent; one important warrant, bridge, or qualification remains compressed. |
| 65–74 | Mixed; a useful core remains materially underdeveloped. |
| 50–64 | Weak; a central burden or live issue is insufficiently discharged despite relevant content. |
| 25–49 | Very weak; the inference, support, or engagement is seriously defective. |
| 0–24 | Non-performance on the assigned issue or no recoverable argumentative route. |

Use 50, 70, 80, and 90 as boundaries, not defaults. A difference of five or more points requires a named source feature or missing bridge.

## Logical coherence — 25%

- **90–100:** explicit, mutually consistent premises and intermediate steps; scope matches the conclusion; the strongest live validity challenge is resolved.
- **80–89:** valid and stable route with one bounded implicit bridge that does not threaten the conclusion.
- **70–79:** intelligible route with one material bridge, scope condition, or ambiguity unresolved.
- **50–69:** a substantial equivocation, unsupported conditional, false dilemma, internal tension, or non sequitur weakens the route.
- **0–49:** self-undermining, plainly invalid at its core, or no usable route to the conclusion.

Evidence weakness belongs primarily under evidence/warrant unless it also creates a distinct inferential failure.

## Evidence and warrant — 20%

- **90–100:** specific, sourceable, representative evidence; defended warrants; serious rival explanations compared.
- **80–89:** strong support with one bounded documentation, independence, base-rate, or rival-hypothesis gap.
- **70–79:** appropriate support exists but a material warrant or verification step is compressed.
- **50–69:** assertion, anecdote, authority, selective examples, or speculative mechanisms carry substantial weight.
- **0–49:** a fact-dependent conclusion is effectively unsupported or contradicted within the locked record.

No inline citation is not itself a deduction. Ask how much verification the inference requires.

## Responsiveness — 20%

Use the locked response class and its permitted range. Coverage and target contact belong here; correctness belongs in coherence and evidence.

### Constructive-opening anchors

- **90–100:** states a complete, motion-level route and its success conditions.
- **80–89:** advances the central route with one component implicit.
- **70–79:** directly establishes a necessary sub-burden.
- **50–69:** topical case-building that is peripheral or only loosely connected to the adopted burden.
- **0–49:** off-motion, burden-substituting, or not a usable constructive contribution.

### Locked response classes

- **Full-answer, 80–100:** every indispensable target component is addressed. Use 90+ only when the answer reaches the decisive premise or inference explicitly; 80–89 when coverage is complete but compressed or indirect.
- **Justified-reframe, 80–100:** the malformed demand is explained and the corrected demand answered. Use 90+ only when both steps are explicit and complete.
- **Diagnostic-defeat, 80–100:** the identified defect would itself defeat the target if correct. Use 90+ only when the decisive defect and its consequence are explicit.
- **Partial-answer, 55–79:** at least one indispensable component is answered and at least one is not. Use 70–79 for the decisive component with a bounded omission; 55–69 for a secondary or limited indispensable component.
- **Relevant-counterargument, 50–69:** contrary material is relevant but does not answer an indispensable target component. Use 60–69 when it materially pressures the target; 50–59 when contact is real but peripheral.
- **Weaker-substitution, 0–49:** use 35–49 for a recognizable but materially weaker reconstruction; 15–34 for substantial issue change; 0–14 for essentially no target contact.

The score may not cross the locked class range. A scorer who believes the class is wrong must record the concern outside the score; the class remains fixed for both passes.

## Relevance and burden — 15%

- **90–100:** completes an adopted burden or decisively defeats an opponent burden, with a proportionate motion-level consequence.
- **80–89:** materially advances the central burden with one bounded bridge or consequence unstated.
- **70–79:** advances a necessary sub-burden without establishing the motion-level consequence.
- **50–69:** topical but peripheral, burden-shifting, or directed at a burden the side did not adopt.
- **0–49:** irrelevant to the motion or dependent on assigning an unadopted positive contrary burden.

Use only locked burden IDs and success criteria. A critic need not prove the contrary unless the critic adopts it. Score the selected span and speaker; do not import later consequences or an allied speaker's distinct theory. A response can be structurally full yet low in burden relevance if it answers a peripheral live target.

## Precision and clarity — 10%

- **90–100:** terms, modality, comparison class, scope, referents, and confidence are stable and auditable.
- **80–89:** clear with one bounded ambiguity that does not alter the inference.
- **70–79:** understandable but compressed or ambiguous at one material point.
- **50–69:** vague quantifiers, unstable terms, unclear referents, or missing distinctions materially obscure evaluation.
- **0–49:** the claim cannot be reconstructed reliably enough to assess.

Fluency, wit, accent, speed, and polish are excluded unless they change recoverability.

## Calibration and charity — 10% combined

Score the subratings separately, then record only their rounded mean as `calibrationCharity`.

### Epistemic calibration

- **90–100:** confidence, modality, and qualifications closely match the supplied warrant; live uncertainty is explicit.
- **80–89:** proportionate with one minor overstatement or omitted caveat.
- **70–79:** one material step beyond the warrant or one necessary caveat is missing.
- **50–69:** substantial overclaiming, selective skepticism, or certainty unsupported by the selected span.
- **0–49:** certainty is radically disproportionate to the available warrant.

### Representational charity

- **90–100:** represents the strongest live alternative accurately, including its decisive qualification, before criticism.
- **80–89:** fair and materially accurate with one minor omission.
- **70–79:** neutral non-engagement where no live alternative is represented or attacked; use **75** as the fixed not-tested anchor.
- **50–69:** a materially weaker reconstruction, omitted decisive qualification, or unsupported motive attribution affects the argument.
- **0–49:** the move depends on a gross caricature of the live position.

Politeness is not charity; abrasiveness is not a deduction. Do not infer intent. If charity is genuinely not tested by the selected act, assign exactly 75 and say so.

## Partial answers, diagnostic moves, and reframes

- A compound target contains only components indispensable to its success. Answering incidental details does not make a response partial.
- A partial answer is not automatically incoherent or irrelevant. Apply a second deduction only for a distinct inference or burden consequence visible in the span.
- A diagnostic question is `diagnostic-defeat` only when its exposed defect would itself defeat the target. Mere clarification, rhetorical questioning, or setup for a later answer does not qualify.
- A reframe is `justified-reframe` only when both the defect in the original demand and the corrected answer are supplied. An unsupported replacement is `weaker-substitution` or another applicable class.
- Structural answer coverage does not imply logical success. A full answer may score poorly in evidence or coherence while remaining 80+ in responsiveness.

## Penalty controls

- Give each defect one primary home; score a second dimension only for a distinct consequence.
- Compatibility is not explanation, and possibility does not establish probability.
- Familiar arguments receive no pedigree bonus.
- Fallacy and bias tags never add numerical penalties.
- Audience reaction, status, confidence of delivery, and style are excluded.
- Missing replies are represented by response links and burden completion, not fabricated counterpart moves.
- The burden adjustment cannot repair inventory, weight, or scoring dissatisfaction.

## Aggregation

Move dimensions retain weights `25/20/20/15/10/10`. Sections are importance-weighted move means. Overall scores are locked section-weighted means plus only an eligible −5 to +5 debate-wide burden residual under the workflow's strengthened exclusion rule. No extra global meta-score is permitted.
