# Slugfester Candidate-Evidence Transport v4.2.21.15

This stage replaces broad sparse Primary A ledgers with one deterministic source-exact excerpt per discovered candidate. It makes no model or audio call and does not change the candidate inventory.

For each candidate, the repository selects 12–90 lexical tokens and at most 450 characters from the candidate's locked inclusive source span. Lexical salience uses proposition, load-bearing reason, and context summary; ties are deterministic. Every candidate is retained. The candidate evidence helps Primary A select and judge the bounded inventory, while evidence for final selected moves is independently rerendered from original events after Primary A supplies its completed proposition and response rationale.

The target is at most 170 KB copied input per Primary A context. A future gate must rerun all three contexts fresh under the already-tested narrow schema. Timeout extension, semantic candidate downselection, predecessor-output reuse, Pass B, audio, scoring, publication, and production mutation remain unauthorized.
