# Slugfester Reassessment Rubric v3.7.1

This audit rubric inherits the v3.7 semantic rules and resolves only anonymous candidate choices.

## Target-family decisions

- Component contact requires explicit propositional contact. Do not propagate generic assent or logical implication across components.
- `inside-locked-target` applies when the response's example is already an instance of the locked target. `distinct-connected-example` requires a distinct case expressly connected to it.
- Any positive component entails `component-contact-precludes-contrary`; `relevant-no-component` requires no positive component.

## Diagnostic and reframe decisions

- A diagnostic defect requires an explicit defect cue, not merely a weak answer.
- A diagnostic consequence requires a distinct clause and an explicit connective describing what follows from the defect.
- A dual-positive reframe requires both an explanation that the governing demand is malformed and a replacement demand, linked explicitly.
- Select relation kinds from the language that actually connects the relevant clauses, not from a nearby connective serving another role.

## Burden decisions

Burden choices are candidate-bound. Select the route or bridge actually advanced by the move. Topic overlap alone is insufficient, and burden contact must not be inferred merely from responsiveness.

For every choice, provide one exact, case-sensitive evidence substring that occurs exactly once in the source excerpt. Expand short cues with adjacent source words when necessary. The rationale must identify the governing positive rule, default, and exclusion.
