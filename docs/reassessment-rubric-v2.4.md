# Slugfester Reassessment Rubric v2.4

This rubric preserves the v2.3 numerical dimensions and weights while changing the structural inputs supplied to scorers. Interaction mode, target packet, target coverage, mechanism flags, burden IDs, and burden relation are locked before numerical scoring.

The current v2.4 held-out gate is classification-only. The numerical anchors below govern only a later gate if annotation reliability passes.

## Fixed weights

`25% logical coherence + 20% evidence/warrant + 20% responsiveness + 15% relevance/burden + 10% precision/clarity + 10% calibration/charity`

Calibration and charity remain separate 0–100 subratings whose rounded mean supplies the combined dimension.

## Shared bands

| Band | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 95–100 | Exceptional, explicit, and unusually complete; little material repair needed. |
| 85–94 | Very strong; the move substantially advances its burden with bounded residual gaps. |
| 75–84 | Strong/competent; one important warrant, bridge, or qualification remains compressed. |
| 65–74 | Mixed; a useful core remains materially underdeveloped. |
| 50–64 | Weak; a central burden or live issue is insufficiently discharged despite relevant content. |
| 25–49 | Very weak; the inference, support, or engagement is seriously defective. |
| 0–24 | Non-performance on the assigned issue or no recoverable argumentative route. |

Logical coherence, evidence/warrant, precision/clarity, epistemic calibration, and representational charity retain the v2.3 anchors and penalty controls.

## Responsiveness — 20%

Use only the inventory-locked interaction mode and adjudicated target coverage.

| Structural lock | Permitted range |
| --- | ---: |
| Constructive | 0–100 using motion and burden contact |
| Responsive + full | 80–100 |
| Responsive + partial | 55–79 |
| Responsive + relevant-nonanswer | 50–69 |
| Responsive + substitution | 0–49 |

For constructive moves: 90–100 states a complete motion-level route; 80–89 advances the central route; 70–79 establishes a necessary sub-burden; 50–69 is topical but peripheral; below 50 lacks usable motion contact.

Coverage is structural. Logical or evidential failure belongs in its own dimension. Diagnostic and reframe flags do not change the range.

## Relevance and burden — 15%

Use only the adjudicated burden relation and its locked burden IDs.

| Burden relation | Permitted range |
| --- | ---: |
| completes | 90–100 |
| advances-central | 80–89 |
| advances-sub-burden | 70–79 |
| topical-peripheral | 50–69 |
| unadopted-or-irrelevant | 0–49 |

A critic does not inherit a positive contrary burden without adopting it. A multi-speaker side does not make every allied theory a shared burden. Score only the atomic selected act, not later completion or cumulative repetition.

## Mechanism controls

`diagnostic` and `reframe` are descriptive flags, not bonuses or penalties. A diagnostic act may be coherent or incoherent, well or poorly evidenced, and full or incomplete. A reframe may clarify the issue or merely substitute one. The numerical dimensions assess those qualities without reclassifying the flags.

## Calibration and charity — 10% combined

Epistemic calibration and representational charity retain the v2.3 anchors. When charity is genuinely not tested by the atomic act, assign exactly 75 and state that it is the fixed not-tested anchor.

## Penalty controls

- Give each defect one primary numerical home; apply a second effect only for a distinct transcript consequence.
- Do not deduct responsiveness for an unsound but structurally complete answer.
- Do not deduct relevance merely because an adopted critic's burden is negative rather than constructive.
- Do not reward diagnostic or reframe flags by themselves.
- Familiar arguments, speaker status, audience reaction, fluency, accent, speed, and confidence of delivery receive no bonus.
- Fallacy and bias tags never add a numerical penalty.
- The debate-wide burden residual cannot repair inventory, annotation, weighting, or scoring dissatisfaction.

## Aggregation and promotion

Move, section, and overall formulas remain unchanged from v2.3. A −5 to +5 burden residual remains zero by default and must pass the strengthened duplicate-exclusion audit. No v2.4 scores enter production unless the held-out annotation gate, a later complete three-debate numerical gate, and the subsequent ten-debate gate all pass with explicit editorial approval.
