Overall assessment

Michael Huemer — Nonphysical souls exist: 76/100

Published strengths

  • The Mary reply gave propositional content to experiential novelty by showing how new evidence excludes alternative qualitative characters.
  • The single-subject clarification avoided treating the soul as a second agent and connected rational, sensory, and emotional states to one bearer.
  • The reembodiment reframe showed that soul persistence need not depend on exact qualitative duplication or eternal physical recurrence.
  • The survival reply consistently distinguished loss of operative capacity from loss of existence.

Published weaknesses

  • The crucial transition from irreducible or unified mental properties to a numerically distinct nonphysical substance remained substantially incomplete.
  • The infinite-time dilemma lacked a justified self-location measure, and its recurrence route did not independently secure identity-preserving survival.
  • The eternal-soul and embodiment proposals remained speculative, with no settled attachment mechanism or persistence criterion.

Graham Oppy — Persons are natural organisms: 84/100

Published strengths

  • The state-acquaintance reply offered a precise identity-theoretic explanation for privileged experiential knowledge without denying Mary's epistemic novelty.
  • Multiscale naturalism blocked the assumption that failure of microphysical definition automatically implies nonphysical ontology.
  • The no-extra-substance diagnosis directly exposed the missing inference from higher-scale causation to an additional bearer.
  • Finite-past and heat-death replies attacked both the temporal premise and the physical recurrence mechanism.
  • Brain-damage evidence supplied a strong empirical case for dependence of manifested human capacities on neural integrity.

Published weaknesses

  • The multiscale and functional accounts remained frameworks rather than detailed explanations of phenomenal character or intrinsic content.
  • Brain dependence did not settle whether a nonoperative bearer could persist after losing every manifested capacity.
  • The fetal-threshold inference moved too quickly from absent neural correlates and mentality to absence of an attached soul.

Phenomenal consciousness and physical explanation

5:06–26:46

Michael Huemer — section score 85/100

Qualia resist physical reduction because their first-person felt character is itself the phenomenon needing explanation, unlike externally reduced phenomena such as heat.

Strongest feature: The argument precisely isolates first-person phenomenal character as the explanatory target and cautiously presents physical description as difficult, preserving the transcript's introspective warrant without overstating impossibility. Principal limitation: Explanatory resistance alone does not establish metaphysical irreducibility, and the heat comparison leaves undeveloped why phenomenal character cannot be accommodated as an autonomous natural property at an appropriate scale. Live burden: The proponent must connect privileged acquaintance to an ontological conclusion while answering the possibility that occupying a physical state supplies knowledge unavailable from detached description. Locked score: The supplied strong and competent band reflects excellent relevance and stable presentation alongside a consequential but unresolved transition from distinctive experience to nonphysical mentality.

Graham Oppy — section score 87/100

Mary's experiential learning remains compatible with identity theory if fully knowing some physical brain states requires personally occupying those states.

Strongest feature: The reply directly explains privileged experiential access within identity theory by distinguishing detached physical identification from the fuller knowledge available only through occupying the relevant brain state. Principal limitation: Compatibility is not yet a complete account of Mary's apparent propositional gain, because the proposal must explain how occupation rules out qualitative possibilities without introducing additional facts. Live burden: The naturalist must characterize the epistemic novelty precisely enough to answer the claim that Mary learns a determinate truth rather than merely acquiring acquaintance, recognition, or practical ability. Locked score: The supplied very strong band reflects exceptional responsiveness, careful conditional calibration, and charitable contact with the objection, while retaining a bounded explanatory obligation concerning novelty.

Intentionality, mental causation, and agency

35:45–87:57

Michael Huemer — section score 72/100

Tree rings become evidence of age only through an observer's interpreting mental state, whereas the rings themselves possess no intrinsic aboutness.

Strongest feature: The tree-ring example lucidly separates derivative evidential significance from intrinsic mental aboutness and preserves a stable, intuitively accessible contrast between interpretation and representation. Principal limitation: The example does not directly answer multiscale naturalism, because a naturalist may accept derivative tree-ring content while offering functional or organismic conditions for intrinsic-seeming attitudes. Live burden: The proponent must explain why mental representation cannot arise from suitably organized natural functions and why its intrinsic status requires a nonphysical subject rather than higher-scale realization. Locked score: The supplied strong and competent band reflects conceptual clarity, appropriate calibration, and useful support for the intentionality challenge, offset by limited responsiveness to the opponent's decisive explanatory framework.

Graham Oppy — section score 81/100

Functionalism can explain intentional attitudes within identity theory, making beliefs and desires less problematic for physicalism than phenomenal consciousness.

Strongest feature: The reply supplies a recognizable physicalist-compatible account of beliefs and desires while candidly distinguishing intentionality from the harder phenomenal-consciousness problem rather than claiming one theory resolves everything. Principal limitation: Invoking functionalism in compressed form leaves unanswered whether causal role captures original aboutness or merely systematizes behavior and relations already interpreted by minded observers. Live burden: The naturalist must explain how functional organization determines specific content, handles error, and grounds representation without presupposing the intentional notions that the account is meant to illuminate. Locked score: The supplied very strong band reflects precise scope, excellent responsiveness, and disciplined concession of the remaining qualia difficulty, despite the absence of a developed content-fixing mechanism in this exchange.

From nonphysical properties to subjects and personal identity

33:33–96:06

Michael Huemer — section score 78/100

Qualia and intentionality motivate property dualism, but personal identity must provide the additional bridge from nonphysical properties to a substance bearing them.

Strongest feature: The move clearly distinguishes property dualism from substance dualism and correctly identifies personal identity as an additional indispensable bridge rather than treating unusual properties as automatically substance-forming. Principal limitation: The suggestion that a separate substance is not much further away understates the substantial need to establish a numerically distinct bearer and principled persistence conditions. Live burden: The proponent must demonstrate why unified mental properties cannot belong to an organism or brain and why their subject remains identical across bodily discontinuity. Locked score: The supplied strong and competent band reflects excellent motion relevance, recoverable conceptual distinctions, and honest acknowledgment of incompleteness, while the central property-to-substance inference remains asserted more confidently than its supporting premises warrant.

Graham Oppy — section score 84/100

Higher-scale or downward-causal organization remains activity occurring in the brain and therefore does not by itself establish an additional mental substance.

Strongest feature: The reply identifies the decisive inferential gap with unusual economy: higher-scale organization and downward causation can remain brain events, so neither automatically supplies a numerically additional bearer. Principal limitation: The categorical absence of mental substances follows within the proposed multiscale story but is only briefly asserted and does not independently exclude every rival interpretation of mental causation. Live burden: The naturalist must explain why unified phenomenal and intentional properties are adequately borne by the organism while addressing arguments that their causal character requires a distinct subject. Locked score: The supplied very strong band reflects diagnostic precision, exceptional responsiveness, and direct contact with an indispensable motion bridge, with evidential development being the principal remaining constraint.

Cosmology, recurrence, and postmortem survival

57:26–94:20

Michael Huemer — section score 73/100

Given time infinite in both directions and only one life per person, present existence allegedly has zero probability, forcing reincarnation or rejection of an infinite past.

Strongest feature: The dilemma explicitly states its two-way infinite-time and single-life assumptions and connects them transparently to a potentially important source of cosmological support for repeated embodiment. Principal limitation: The zero-probability inference lacks a justified probability measure over temporal locations, since actual existence at one time need not result from uniform random sampling across infinity. Live burden: The proponent must define the self-location reference class, defend its measure, and then show that recurrence preserves personal identity rather than merely reproducing physical or qualitative patterns. Locked score: The supplied mixed band reflects substantial relevance and an intelligible conditional structure, but also a fundamental measure-theoretic gap and an incomplete bridge from recurrence to survival of the same subject involved.

Graham Oppy — section score 87/100

Current cosmology comparatively favors a finite past and infinite future, while expansion and entropy independently weaken the proposed recurrence mechanism.

Strongest feature: The reply attacks both supports of the cosmological route by challenging the two-way infinite premise and separately invoking expansion and entropy against recurrent conditions. Principal limitation: Comparative favoring by contemporary physics does not establish metaphysical impossibility, and the compressed presentation provides limited documentation or systematic comparison with nonstandard cosmological alternatives. Live burden: The naturalist must keep the conclusion appropriately comparative while showing that the proponent cannot legitimately rely on an unsupported infinite past or physical recurrence within the argument offered. Locked score: The supplied very strong band reflects exceptional diagnostic contact, stable separation of distinct issues, and calibrated appeal to scientific plausibility rather than certainty, with model dependence remaining a bounded qualification.

Live burden: The proponent must explain why preexisting subjects become associated with particular organisms, what determines eligibility, and how embodiment preserves identity rather than generating an arbitrary pairing. Locked score: The supplied mixed band reflects direct relevance to survival possibility and appropriately tentative mechanism language, offset by weak warrant, unstable threshold specification, and failure to answer the selected resemblance-based personal-identity objection.

Live burden: The naturalist must explain why the dependence pattern favors organismic identity over an enabling-interface model while preserving the distinction between functional loss and metaphysical nonexistence. Locked score: The supplied strong and competent band reflects substantial empirical warrant, clear burden relevance, and fair acknowledgment of uncertainty, tempered by partial responsiveness and an incomplete inference from dependence to ontology.
