Overall assessment

David Wood — Cognitive-reliability critique: 81/100

Published strengths

  • Clearly distinguished an internal critique of naturalism from the separate task of proving supernaturalism.
  • Exposed the unsupported transition from adaptive social cooperation to reliability about comprehensive worldview questions.
  • Maintained pressure on the scope of public correction by asking how locally testable success warrants broader metaphysical confidence.
  • Offered a coherent evidential route whereby alleged supernatural causes could be assessed through observable natural effects.

Published weaknesses

  • The physical-causation argument treated neural realization and rational responsiveness as incompatible without establishing that crucial premise.
  • The fitness argument sometimes moved from selection not directly targeting truth to an excessively broad absence of cognitive warrant.
  • The falsifiability challenge generalized hypothetical reinterpretations by some naturalists without demonstrating systematic evidential insulation.

Aron Ra — Evidence-centered naturalism: 79/100

Published strengths

  • Distinguished provisional acceptance of the known physical world from an unsupported universal denial of every immaterial possibility.
  • Grounded the burden for supernatural additions in independent, repeatable, objective verification.
  • Presented sensory reliance and naturalistic conclusions as provisional and corrigible rather than certain.
  • Specified contrary documentation as relevant evidence and expressly allowed that adequate verification would require reconsideration.

Published weaknesses

  • The social-evolution reply explained interpersonal dependability but never bridged that capacity to abstract metaphysical reliability.
  • The appeal to unavoidable sensory reliance did not fully answer the challenge concerning rational warrant for worldview-level conclusions.
  • The late rejection of determinism remained largely assertive and supplied no developed account of physically realized rational causation.

Evolutionary Fitness and Truth-Tracking Cognition

26:58–44:33

David Wood — section score 84/100

Selection directly favors survival and reproduction, not abstract truth, leaving an unresolved warrant gap when evolved faculties address theology, ethics, epistemology, or metaphysics.

Strongest feature: The move identifies the debate's central domain-extension problem by contrasting faculties selected for practical survival with confidence in abstract theological, ethical, epistemological, and metaphysical conclusions. Principal limitation: Selection's failure to target truth directly does not establish that adaptive cognition has almost no reliability, because accurate representation, cultural learning, and institutional correction may indirectly connect fitness with truth. Live burden: The critique must estimate how far those indirect connections extend, identify which abstract domains exceed them, and show why public checking cannot adequately repair the resulting uncertainty without presupposing the reliability under dispute. Locked score: The supplied strong and competent band reflects outstanding relevance and a genuine explanatory gap while retaining substantial reservations about overstatement, evidential support, and insufficient engagement with indirect truth-tracking mechanisms.

Aron Ra — section score 76/100

Social evolution favored empathy, cooperation, trustworthiness, and dependable partners, giving selection a practical reason to preserve some reliable interpersonal cognition.

Strongest feature: The reply supplies an intelligible evolutionary reason for preserving dependable social dispositions by connecting empathy, cooperation, trustworthiness, and reliable partnership with differential success across generations. Principal limitation: Practical interpersonal dependability does not establish reliability concerning comprehensive metaphysical propositions, and the move never constructs the necessary bridge from successful cooperation to abstract truth-sensitive cognition. Live burden: The evidence-centered defense must explain how locally adaptive representational capacities combine with language, criticism, measurement, and cumulative institutions to support conclusions outside immediate social environments, while acknowledging remaining domain-specific error. Locked score: The supplied strong and competent band appropriately reflects relevant contact, coherent social explanation, and charitable engagement alongside partial responsiveness, limited evidence, and an unresolved extension to worldview-level reliability.

Objective Evidence, Experience, and Cognitive Correction

59:58–103:22

David Wood — section score 81/100

Failed supernatural claims within one person's experience cannot settle every reported miracle, particularly when the debate independently questions that evaluator's cognitive reliability.

Strongest feature: The reply correctly limits extrapolation from one person's encounters with failed supernatural claims and reconnects the evaluation of those encounters to the debate's broader dispute about cognitive reliability. Principal limitation: Its omniscience language exaggerates the opponent's position, because repeated investigative failures can justify provisional skepticism without purporting to survey or conclusively dismiss every reported miracle. Live burden: The critique must identify credible unexamined cases, explain why their documentation survives ordinary alternative explanations, and show that the opponent's prior is excessively restrictive rather than merely informed by accumulated disconfirmation. Locked score: The supplied strong and competent band reflects direct contact with an evidential overgeneralization and an important scope qualification, balanced against broad terminology, rhetorical overstatement, and limited affirmative evidence for the wider reports invoked.

Aron Ra — section score 81/100

Live burden: The defense must explain why unavoidable reliance becomes epistemically responsible reliance through calibration, predictive success, cross-checking, and error correction, then show how those resources support abstract conclusions rather than perception alone. Falsifiability and Evidence Beyond Nature

Strongest feature: The move poses a concrete disconfirmation test by asking whether even striking apparent manifestations of divine agency would automatically receive an alien or otherwise natural reinterpretation. Principal limitation: Hypothetical reactions attributed to some naturalists do not establish that naturalism generally excludes supernatural explanations, because comparative preference for a natural explanation may reflect evidential assessment rather than prior insulation. Live burden: The challenge must specify an observational pattern whose likelihood differs meaningfully between rival hypotheses and show that naturalists would reject the better-supported explanation solely because of its classification, not because agency identification remains underdetermined. Locked score: The supplied mixed band reflects a clear, relevant falsifiability concern with proportionate conditional force, while retaining substantial reservations about target generalization, evidential specificity, and limited direct responsiveness.
