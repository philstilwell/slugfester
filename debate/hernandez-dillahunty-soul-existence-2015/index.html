Overall assessment

Eric Hernandez — Substance dualism: 68/100

Published strengths

  • The opening distinguishes correlations from identity by comparing mental and neural properties, directly establishing the central nonphysicality route with strong responsiveness and clear burden contact.
  • The unified-subject argument connects one visual field to one experiencer, explaining why aggregated processing may not exhaust conscious unity or identify its bearer.
  • The brain-instrument replies engage injury evidence directly, explaining impaired expression through damaged access rather than denying observed dependence of personality and ability.
  • The closing integrates intentionality, unity, persistence, and agency into one metaphysical case, maintaining clear relevance to whether a soul grounds disconnected personal capacities.

Published weaknesses

  • The irreducibility argument assumes components lacking beliefs cannot collectively realize beliefs, but it does not establish why organization could never generate a novel system-level property.
  • The hand-raising demonstration shows responsive behavior but does not distinguish libertarian choice from caused performance, leaving its inference vulnerable to the computer comparison.
  • The instrument analogy remains compatible with brain damage but supplies no independent discriminator showing damaged access rather than altered mentality, limiting evidential force.

Matt Dillahunty — Evidential skepticism: 82/100

Published strengths

  • The necessity-versus-compatibility distinction redirects anecdotes and unexplained experience toward the affirmative burden, preventing a merely available soul interpretation from becoming automatic evidence of the soul's actual existence.
  • Split-brain and injury comparisons connect changes in knowledge, personality, memory, and behavior to neural organization, pressuring claims of one indivisible, unaffected personal subject.
  • The computer-response counterexample answers the hand-raising demonstration by showing that identical outward compliance can occur without the freedom the demonstration purportedly establishes, so the observed action alone remains inconclusive.
  • The closing consolidates verification, entity individuation, detectable interaction, and physical alternatives into a direct, specific demonstration burden that answers the affirmative synthesis.

Published weaknesses

  • The emergence reply preserves a physical alternative but offers limited positive support for emergence itself, so it blocks necessity more effectively than it explains experience.
  • The changing-label account is practical and responsive, yet it leaves underdeveloped why continuity judgments remain warranted across substantial physical and psychological alteration.
  • The claim that causality supports rationality is compressed and does not fully distinguish causal responsiveness from the stronger epistemic reliability challenged by the affirmative.

Principal limitation: The argument establishes a difference in descriptions more readily than a difference in substances, while its supporting excerpt does not independently exclude physical realization of mental properties. Live burden: The move must show that the alleged property differences are genuinely ontological and cannot arise from perspective, organization, or differing levels of description within one physical system.
