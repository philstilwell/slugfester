Scope neglect

Scope neglect: Failing to adjust judgment in proportion to the scale, number, or magnitude of what is being discussed.

How to interpret this label

A label identifies a specific problem in the reasoning, not a judgment about the person. It adds no separate numerical penalty. See the assessment standards.

Richard Swinburne vs Bart Ehrman — God and Suffering · 2009

Richard Swinburne · Natural evil and victim benefit · 38:05

Swinburne argues natural suffering creates otherwise unavailable choices for sufferers and observers, whose repeated responses form character and advance possible holiness.

Treats character-forming opportunities as justificatory without proportionately weighing natural suffering across victims, severity, and distribution.

Luuk Vandeweghe · Historical sources, authorship, and memory · 1:08:54

Vandeweghe argues that memory errors typically concern peripheral details, whereas meaningful identity-forming experiences remain stable enough to preserve the central content of reported miracle encounters.

Vandeweghe extrapolates stable three-month recall to miracle memories transmitted across the much longer Gospel period.
