Begging the question

Begging the question: Assuming, directly or indirectly, the very point that needs to be established, so the conclusion is smuggled into the premises.

How to interpret this label

A label identifies a specific problem in the reasoning, not a judgment about the person. It adds no separate numerical penalty. See the assessment standards.

Dan Barker · Morality, human nature, and salvation · 1:23:07

Craig uses a fly-on-a-blank-wall analogy to argue that distant life-permitting regions do not reduce the improbability of hitting our isolated local range within a selected neighborhood.

The fly analogy assumes uniform random aim, a fixed local reference class, and an independently specified target.
