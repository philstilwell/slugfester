Special pleading

Special pleading: Applying an exception or different standard to one side of the argument without giving a principled reason for the difference.

How to interpret this label

A label identifies a specific problem in the reasoning, not a judgment about the person. It adds no separate numerical penalty. See the assessment standards.

William Lane Craig · Fine-tuning and design · 15:09

Craig defines fine-tuning neutrally, distinguishes constants from initial quantities, and argues that exceptionally narrow life-permitting ranges favor design over physical necessity or chance.
