Appeal to authority

Appeal to authority: Relying on a person or institution's status as if it settles the argument, especially when the relevant evidence, expertise, or consensus has not been shown.

How to interpret this label

A label identifies a specific problem in the reasoning, not a judgment about the person. It adds no separate numerical penalty. See the assessment standards.
